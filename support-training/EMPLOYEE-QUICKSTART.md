# Your first steps in NEVORA

1. **Open your own invitation.** Use the invitation email requested by your workplace Owner. Keep the link private. If it is missing or cannot be used, ask your Owner to check status and arrange a new invitation if needed. Do not forward the full link or invitation proof to support.
2. **Choose your own password.** Enter and confirm it privately, following the on-screen rules. After activation, keep your Employee ID and choose Continue to sign in. Sign in using that ID and your password. If activation cannot be confirmed, ask your Owner to check account status; your account may already be active.
3. **Check your schedule.** Open Schedule / My schedule (`/app/schedule`). Check the week, dates, times and location for your own shifts. Follow any published or unavailable notice. An empty day in an unpublished schedule does not yet mean a day off; ask your manager.
4. **Tell your workplace when you can work.** Open Availability (`/app/availability`), including through Requests where shown. Check whether you are editing your usual week or one specific week. Save/submit as prompted and read the confirmation and any deadline. Contact your manager if the week is locked or information is wrong.
5. **Make a request if available.** Open Requests (`/app/requests/hub`), choose the available request type, check your dates and submit. Review its status; submitting a request does not approve it. Your manager handles workplace decisions.

For sign-in help, use Forgot access (`/forgot-access`). If you cannot use your registered email, ask your manager or Owner for identity-checked recovery. Email **support@thevelani.com** for technical problems or privacy concerns. Describe what happened and your device/app version; conceal personal information in screenshots. Never send passwords, invitation proofs, verification/recovery codes, bank details or payroll documents to support.
