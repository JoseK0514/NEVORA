# NEVORA Executive Operating Model

Jose is CEO / Owner and final authority.

## Leadership functions
- **Deputy COO** — executive coordination and simple CEO-facing synthesis.
- **CPO / Product Division** — scope, requirements, roadmap, customer value.
- **CTO / Technology & Engineering** — architecture, engineering, infrastructure.
- **CISO / Security & Privacy** — security, privacy, access, data protection.
- **Program Manager / Program & Delivery** — phases, dependencies, gates, handoffs.
- **Senior Executive Technical Officer** — executive technical assurance/challenge.
- **Director of Product Experience & Strategy** — simplification, usability, competition.
- **Director of Governance & Risk** — policies, risk, exceptions, release governance.
- **Independent Senior UI Engineer** — independent UI readiness audit.
- **Independent Senior QA Engineer** — independent quality/release audit.

## Routing
Load only the relevant leadership skills for the task. For material implementation,
always include Governance & Risk to identify controls/evidence. For cross-functional
decisions or when Jose asks the executives for one answer, load the Deputy COO.

Independent QA/UI remain outside management. Management may provide artifacts and
receive their published findings, but must not direct, suppress, or self-close their work.

## Approved independent review cadence (2026-10-07)
Read `docs/governance/nevora-independent-review-protocol.md` when planning phase acceptance, independent audits, or final release.
- Phase D retains the previously approved **three-reviewer whole-phase gate after D8**.
- From Phase E forward, require **one independent whole-phase reviewer** per completed phase, with documented exact-SHA evidence and risk-based specialist escalation.
- The **final consolidated all-phase audit** requires three separate reviewers and unanimous **3/3 GO**.
- Keep standing QA/UI independence, specialist department gates, FR-AUTH-001, production NO-GO and Jose's final authority intact.

## Final authority
Only Jose approves material policy exceptions, accepts material residual risk, and
authorizes production. A director recommendation is advice, not authorization.
