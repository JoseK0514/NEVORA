# NEVORA Manager and Lead web guide

This guide describes current development web access. Your active role, current
Owner-assigned scope, module switches and separate payroll grants determine what
you can use. See the [guide index](README.md) for release boundaries.

## Check which access you have

| Access | Current operational boundary |
| --- | --- |
| Manager with company scope | Company operational pages, subject to each workflow's current guards |
| Manager with location scope | Managed-location Home, schedule and limited team view; bounded draft-shift changes where offered |
| Manager without a current assignment | Operational access remains unavailable; ask an Owner to assign scope |
| Lead | Own schedule, availability, requests, time and published pay statements; no Manager review authority |

Payroll review and approval are separate grants. A location-scoped Manager does
not gain company totals, contacts or financial records by opening a saved link.
An Owner controls management assignments. A promotion, reactivation or later
scope change can invalidate access that was available earlier.

## Company-scoped Manager workflow

Start at **Home** and **Things to do**. Open the current record before acting;
old dashboard counts and earlier receipts are not current authorization.

For planning, open **Schedule**, choose the week and location, and review
availability and scheduling rules. Create or edit eligible assignments, review
drafts and publish through the offered review. **Copy previous week** and
**Week templates** are planning aids; inspect their resulting assignments and
warnings. Employees follow the published revision, not an unpublished edit.
The Owner controls the trades-and-coverage policy.

Use **Time off** and **Shift requests** for decisions currently permitted to
your account. Check the people, dates, status and current rules. A coworker's
acceptance does not necessarily finish a request; it can still need management
approval. Review the resulting status and schedule after a decision.

**Team** provides employee records within company authority. Current forms may
allow permitted employee changes, but you cannot create or edit another Owner
as if they were an ordinary team member. Account invitations and Manager scope
assignments remain Owner controls. **Help someone sign in** supports authorized
assisted recovery; the person keeps their password and private verification
material.

Review actual punches and correction requests in **Time** / **Timecards**.
Use **Insights** and **Time exports** only for the period you intend. Planned
shifts do not establish recorded attendance or final pay. Owner-only company
settings and Clocker administration remain with the Owner.

## Location-scoped Manager workflow

1. On **Your workspace**, confirm the listed managed locations.
2. Open **Schedule**, choose a managed location and week, and review its shifts.
   **Add shift** and editable draft forms appear only when the current source
   and permissions allow them. Use only the offered employee/position choices.
3. Follow a read-only message when a protected or published shift requires
   company-wide access. Escalate publishing, company rules and protected changes
   to the Owner or a company-scoped Manager.
4. Open **Team at your locations** to review eligible people. This view omits
   contact details and employee editing; request those changes through the Owner.

Company-wide totals, request review, reporting, exports and employee management
are not granted merely by managing one location. Separate payroll grants may
offer additional payroll pages, with their own coverage checks.

## Payroll access, when granted

If **Payroll** is offered, follow the current prepare/review/fix workflow within
your grant. Review permission does not imply approval permission. Payroll
configuration remains Owner-only, and an object outside your financial scope
remains unavailable. Approval and sharing statements are separate actions.
Payment-instruction files do not move money; the employer uses its payment
system. See the [Owner guide](owner.md) for the workflow's review boundaries.

## Lead workflow

Use the [Employee guide](employee.md) for your own activation, shifts,
availability, requests, recorded time and pay statements. Lead navigation may
use **Hours** or place personal time under **Pay**, while Employee navigation
uses **Time**. Your own records remain the scope in either layout.

The access role **Lead**, an operational position and being the designated lead
on a shift are separate facts. None grants employee editing, company settings,
schedule publication, time-off approval, payroll approval or Rondo access.

## If access or a save changes

Reconnect and refresh current information. Stop on access-denied or changed-source
messages; ask an Owner to confirm your assignment. Use a workflow's **check
outcome** or recovery action after an unconfirmed save rather than creating a
second request. A missing or unavailable read is not a confirmed empty result.
Use **More → Help** for an app problem and keep private employee/pay data out of
support messages.
