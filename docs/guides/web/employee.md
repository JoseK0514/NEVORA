# NEVORA Employee web guide

This guide covers the development web app on a computer or mobile browser. Your
company's enabled modules determine which tools appear. Native apps and native
notifications have separate availability and acceptance. See the
[guide index](README.md) for the distribution's source boundary.

## Activate and sign in

1. Obtain your company sign-in address and a private account invitation from
   your Owner. Open the invitation from your email in a browser with JavaScript
   enabled.
2. On **Activate account**, choose and confirm your password. After confirmed
   activation, keep the displayed **Employee ID**; use it with your password to
   sign in. Activation does not itself prove you are signed in.
3. If the link is invalid or expired, ask the Owner for a new invitation. If
   activation is unconfirmed, ask them to check account status; try normal
   sign-in with your known ID before requesting another invitation.

Keep your invitation, password, recovery code and passkeys private. Use the
sign-in help/recovery flow if needed. Your manager can help through an authorized
recovery process without learning your password.

## Find your work

Employee navigation offers **Home**, **Schedule**, **Time**, **Requests** and,
when enabled, **Pay**. Open the avatar / **More** for other tools such as
**Updates**, **Notifications**, **Profile and settings** and **Help**.
Leads have the same personal workflows, with some different navigation labels;
see [Manager and Lead](manager-lead.md).

Live **Updates** communications remain under a rollout hold. If that destination
is unavailable, follow your employer's existing communication channel.

On Home, check your current shift information and pending work. Open **Schedule**
and select the intended week. Review the date, location, start/end time and any
instructions. You see published work. A manager's draft or an old notification
does not establish your latest assignment.

If the week asks you to confirm review, acknowledge the current published
revision after reading it. Acknowledgement records that review; it is not a
clock-in. If a schedule changes, check the new information and any new review
request. Calendar subscriptions, when offered, are a convenience: return to the
app for current authoritative details.

## Tell management when you can work

Open **Availability** from Schedule or the available menu.

- **Your usual week** supplies your recurring available hours.
- **Change one week** edits only the selected schedule week.
- Review every day, save/submit and check the resulting status. An intentionally
  unavailable whole week is different from never submitting that week.
- Respect the displayed deadline. Once locked, the submitted weekly information
  is preserved; contact management about a later change.

Availability tells management when you can work. It does not approve time off or
change an already assigned shift by itself.

## Request time off or a shift change

Use **Requests** for your time-off records and request status. Submit the dates
and information the form requests, then check whether the result is waiting,
approved or declined. Use withdrawal only when the current record offers it.
Submitting a request is not approval.

Open a shift's offered options from Schedule or **Shift requests** to request a
swap, offer it for cover or report **Unable to work**. Review the exact shift,
other person and exchange terms before sending. For incoming work, use the
offered **Accept**, **Decline** or **Claim** control. Some requests still need
management approval or fail current eligibility checks. Follow the final status
and published schedule; do not assume another person's agreement completes it.

Check open shifts and the reasons shown when a shift cannot be claimed. Cancel
or withdraw only while the current record permits it. For an urgent absence,
also follow your employer's contact procedure; an app request does not guarantee
that a manager has seen it.

## Record and check your time

Use your location's enrolled **Clocker** to record punches and breaks under your
employer's procedure. Protect your Clocker PIN. The personal time page displays
recorded punches; it does not provide a universal phone clock-in feature.

Open **Time** / **My time** to review clock status, today's entries, earlier
records and past timecards. Check dates, location, punch times and breaks. An
open entry is still in progress. Scheduled hours and elapsed time are different.

When a record is wrong, open it and use the offered correction request. Describe
the actual issue, submit once and review its waiting/decided state. Requesting a
correction does not immediately replace the approved time record. Contact your
manager when the required action is unavailable.

## View pay and manage your account

When payroll and pay statements are enabled, open **Pay** / **My pay**. Download
only your own published pay statements and check the period and payday. An
approved payroll may not yet have been shared. A payday label or statement does
not confirm a bank deposit; your employer's payment system sends payment.
Ask management about hours, amounts or payment timing.

**Profile and settings** shows your personal account tools. Passkeys, calendar
and device alerts are offered only when configured. Browser notification consent,
device enrollment and actual notification delivery are different states. Keep
checking Schedule and Requests even if no alert arrives. Sign out on shared
devices. If **Delete my account** is offered, read its consequences and the
current policy before confirming; a received deletion request is not completed
erasure, and withdrawal is a Support procedure rather than an in-app undo.

## When something is missing or unconfirmed

Return online and refresh. A loading, paused or unavailable view does not mean
there are no shifts, punches, requests or statements. After an uncertain action,
use its recovery/check control before repeating it. Use **More → Help** for
technical support; contact your employer for work decisions. Include a safe page
description and approximate time, without passwords, codes, bank details or pay
documents.
