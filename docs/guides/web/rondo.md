# Rondo web guide

Rondo is the company operations assistant in the current Owner web workflow.
The integrated page and navigation are still labelled **AI manager**. This guide
uses Rondo's product name without claiming a displayed UI rename or Manager
rollout. The [guide index](README.md) identifies the development distribution.

## Availability and access

Only a current Owner with company authority can open this page. It is hidden or
unavailable when the feature is disabled. Model investigation also requires
approved data use, a configured supported provider/model, server-side credentials
and a bounded daily limit. Opening the page does not prove the model provider is
authenticated or healthy. A local test does not establish hosted acceptance.

If the page is unavailable, ask the authorized operator to check configuration.
Do not paste an API key into chat or switch accounts to gain access. Paid model
use requires its own approved budget. The public website adviser is a separate
surface with different information and capabilities.

## Investigate a specific decision

1. Open **AI manager** and review **Live briefing**, its observation time and
   the company/date. Use **Refresh history** when returning to an old tab or
   when current information is hidden or unavailable.
2. Ask a bounded question: identify the date, location and decision. For example,
   “What coverage is still missing tomorrow, and what options fit current
   availability?” or “Which recorded-time exceptions need review for this
   period?”
3. Read the evidence and limits with the recommendation. Separate recorded facts,
   assumptions, saved goals and missing sources. Check decisive records in their
   normal pages before taking a material action.
4. If facts change, refresh and investigate again. An old conversation is not a
   live company record, and a partial coverage result is not a complete company
   finding.

Current bounded tools cover workforce briefing, published staffing, coverage and
availability investigation, hypothetical coverage plans, operational insights,
attendance/payroll review and fixed-source legal research. They do not connect
every company system. Sales, banking, inventory and other absent integrations
cannot be treated as known facts.

Ask for the decisive evidence, remaining uncertainty, practical fallback and how
to verify the result. A search that finds no complete plan may have reached its
limits; it is not automatically proof that no solution exists. A legal source
or payroll review does not establish universal applicability or compliance.

## Save intent explicitly

Use **Owner priorities → Save priorities** for information you want considered
in later investigations. Where available, use **Saved goals** and tasks to keep
an objective and notes, select a task for an investigation, or update its state.
Read the currently saved values before resolving a conflicting edit.

This private context belongs to the current Owner. A saved objective is intent,
not new operational evidence, another person's acceptance or permission to act.
Chat wording alone does not silently change a schedule or grant authority.
Refresh saved context when it becomes outdated and avoid unnecessary personal or
sensitive material.

## Review and save a coverage draft

1. Use the offered coverage comparison or **Prepare coverage options** workflow.
   Compare one hypothetical plan at a time and confirm actual availability with
   the people involved.
2. Open **Review draft changes**. Check every person, location, date, time,
   position and designated lead, the remaining coverage shortfall and the review
   expiry. Partial scope requires a separate full-schedule review.
3. If you want those exact additions, select the acknowledgement and choose
   **Save … draft assignments**. Current rules and authority are checked again;
   a stale or blocked review needs a fresh comparison.
4. Read the save receipt, then **Check progress** and **Open this schedule**.
   A recorded save and the current presence of its assignments are different
   observations. Later edits can change the plan.
5. Review and publish through Schedule as a separate Owner decision. Saving these
   drafts creates no schedule publication or employee notification, and does not
   confirm acceptance, attendance or solved business coverage.

If the save is uncertain, choose **Check original request**. Preserve that
request; starting another save can duplicate intended work. A cancellation or
lost response does not prove that server work or provider use never occurred.

## Understand the action boundary

Rondo's model tools investigate read-only company evidence. Explicit coverage
commands can save the Owner-reviewed draft described above. The current workflow
does not independently publish schedules, notify people, pay employees, file
taxes or run unattended company management. The Owner still reviews the normal
workflow's evidence and outcome.

For source-level capability limits and future direction, consult the
[Rondo operating contract](../../product/rondo-operating-contract.md). Its dated
evaluation records are historical evidence, not certification of every answer or
this distribution's hosted provider state. Report unavailable or contradictory
results through **Help**, without forwarding private conversation or employee
records unnecessarily.
