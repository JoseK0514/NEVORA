# Rondo web guide

Rondo is the company operations assistant in the NEVORA web app. Owners open
it as **Rondo AI** from the sidebar or **More**. Managers have their own
read-only Rondo AI view when the Manager switch is on (see
[Rondo AI for Managers](#rondo-ai-for-managers)). The [guide index](README.md)
identifies the development distribution.

## Availability and access

Only a current Owner with company authority can open the Owner page. It is hidden or
unavailable when the feature is disabled. Model investigation also requires
approved data use, a configured supported provider/model, server-side credentials
and a bounded daily limit. Opening the page does not prove the model provider is
authenticated or healthy. A local test does not establish hosted acceptance.

If the page is unavailable, ask the authorized operator to check configuration.
Do not paste an API key into chat or switch accounts to gain access. Paid model
use requires its own approved budget. The public website adviser is a separate
surface with different information and capabilities.

## Investigate a specific decision

1. Open **Rondo AI** and review **Live briefing**, its observation time and
   the company/date. Use **Refresh history** when returning to an old tab or
   when current information is hidden or unavailable.
2. Ask a bounded question: identify the date, location and decision. For example,
   “What coverage is still missing tomorrow, and what options fit current
   availability?” or “Which recorded-time exceptions need review for this
   period?”
3. Read the evidence and limits with the recommendation. Separate recorded facts,
   assumptions, saved goals and missing sources. Check decisive records in their
   normal pages before taking a material action.
4. If facts change, refresh and investigate again. An old conversation is not a
   live company record, and a partial coverage result is not a complete company
   finding.

Current bounded tools cover workforce briefing, published staffing, coverage and
availability investigation, hypothetical coverage plans, operational insights,
attendance/payroll review and fixed-source legal research. They do not connect
every company system. Sales, banking, inventory and other absent integrations
cannot be treated as known facts.

Ask for the decisive evidence, remaining uncertainty, practical fallback and how
to verify the result. A search that finds no complete plan may have reached its
limits; it is not automatically proof that no solution exists. A legal source
or payroll review does not establish universal applicability or compliance.

## Save intent explicitly

Use **Owner priorities → Save priorities** for information you want considered
in later investigations. Where available, use **Saved goals** and tasks to keep
an objective and notes, select a task for an investigation, or update its state.
Read the currently saved values before resolving a conflicting edit.

This private context belongs to the current Owner. A saved objective is intent,
not new operational evidence, another person's acceptance or permission to act.
Chat wording alone does not silently change a schedule or grant authority.
Refresh saved context when it becomes outdated and avoid unnecessary personal or
sensitive material.

## Review and save a coverage draft

1. Use the offered coverage comparison or **Prepare coverage options** workflow.
   Compare one hypothetical plan at a time and confirm actual availability with
   the people involved.
2. Open **Review draft changes**. Check every person, location, date, time,
   position and designated lead, the remaining coverage shortfall and the review
   expiry. Partial scope requires a separate full-schedule review.
3. If you want those exact additions, select the acknowledgement and choose
   **Save … draft assignments**. Current rules and authority are checked again;
   a stale or blocked review needs a fresh comparison.
4. Read the save receipt, then **Check progress** and **Open this schedule**.
   A recorded save and the current presence of its assignments are different
   observations. Later edits can change the plan.
5. Review and publish through Schedule as a separate Owner decision. Saving these
   drafts creates no schedule publication or employee notification, and does not
   confirm acceptance, attendance or solved business coverage.

If the save is uncertain, choose **Check original request**. Preserve that
request; starting another save can duplicate intended work. A cancellation or
lost response does not prove that server work or provider use never occurred.

## Maintain company knowledge

Open **Company knowledge** from Rondo AI. This library belongs to the company
and is shared with its authorized Owners; it is separate from private Owner
priorities and goals.

1. Write the **Business profile**, or choose **Add document**. Paste text or load
   a TXT/Markdown file. PDF and Word ingestion are not included in this workflow.
2. Choose **Save** (or **Save draft** on desktop), then review the exact saved version and its text.
   A saved draft is not current guidance. Choose **Publish for Rondo** deliberately
   after checking the content, effective dates and review date.
3. Open a version to inspect its private citation. Old versions remain historical;
   a draft, future, ended, overdue or withdrawn source must not be treated as
   freshly confirmed business truth.
4. **Archive** withdraws the entry from current guidance while keeping versions
   readable. **Delete** requires the acknowledgement and removes retained source
   text for every Owner. Existing chat text and text previously sent to a model
   provider have separate retention.

Editing the library needs no model key. Using it in answers requires the separate
knowledge-retrieval configuration and an available model investigation service.
Publication makes text eligible guidance; it is not a new scheduling rule or
permission for Rondo to act. Treat documents as evidence and verify conflicts.
If a save is not confirmed, use **Check again** for that original operation.

## Compare and approve a full week

When available, open **Weekly plan** from Rondo AI. Select the week and location
scope, then **Compare options**. **Fill open gaps** keeps current shifts.
**Rebuild generated drafts** may replace earlier generated drafts; protected,
manual and published work stays protected by the scheduling rules.

1. Compare each option's coverage, additions, replacements, remaining gaps,
   excluded locations and available history. Partial information and a search
   that stopped early are different limits. Scheduled hours are business planning
   minutes, not worked time or a priced labor budget.
2. Choose **Review option A** or **Review option B**. Review every proposed added
   and removed shift, person, date, time, location, position and lead assignment,
   plus remaining gaps and issues. Each option is a separate complete proposal;
   selecting pieces of different options is not supported.
3. Select **I reviewed all proposed changes and remaining gaps**, then **Save as
   draft**, to approve that exact set. The server checks current source, rules,
   authority and review expiry again. If something changed, compare and review
   again. It does not silently choose another plan for your approval.
4. Choose **Decline** to reject an option without changing the schedule. A save
   writes the complete approved set atomically as drafts. It does not publish
   the schedule or notify staff. Publish later through Schedule after review.
5. If an approval is not confirmed, use **Check again**. Reload recovery checks
   the original approval; **Send again** retains its identity and cannot save it
   twice. Keep the original operation rather than starting a new approval to
   recover a lost reply.

**What was saved** is the original receipt. **The schedule now** is a separate
current observation: later changes, removal or publication can alter it. Coverage
continues to count the locations included in the reviewed plan, with changed or
unmeasurable scope identified. While weekly planning is off, authorized links to
previously saved plans can still show a minimal receipt, without a new comparison
or schedule mutation. Review details hide when the Owner steps away or the short
read lease ends, and must be read again with current authority.

## Rondo AI for Managers

A Manager sees **Rondo AI** when the adviser switch and the Manager switch are
both on and the Manager has a current company or location assignment. The view
reads the schedule and changes nothing.

1. Choose a location you manage and a week.
2. Choose one of four questions: published shifts, drafts, scheduled hours or
   released shifts. Rondo shows the figure, what it counts, the location, the
   week and the time it checked.
3. Read **Published shifts and planned hours** for each day's published shifts,
   drafts, released shifts and planned hours.

Hours are planned time from the schedule; timecards show hours worked. Released
shifts followed an approved “Can’t work” request and are not in the draft or
published counts. A Manager cannot type a question, open business records,
company knowledge or Owner priorities, or read a location outside their
assignment. Answers hide when you switch away; choose **Show again** to read them
with current access.

## Understand the action boundary

Rondo investigates company evidence. Your reviewed coverage commands can save
drafts; the model does not publish schedules, notify people, pay employees,
file taxes or run unattended management. Review the normal workflow’s result.
See the [operating contract](../../product/rondo-operating-contract.md).
Report unavailable or contradictory results through **Help** with minimal private
context. Past evaluations do not certify current answers or provider access.


## Business records and figures, when enabled

The Development source includes **Business records**, a complete version review,
**Main records** for the coverage map, and **Check figures**. A current Company
Owner and the appropriate parent/import/retrieval flags are required. The flags
remain off by default; source inclusion does not activate hosted access, a bank,
accounting or POS connector, or paid inference.

Import JSON or explicitly map CSV fields, review the full bounded response and
its findings, then explicitly publish that exact version. Review and acknowledge
a coverage map separately. Figures retain currency, period, source, freshness and
missing-scope limits. Receivables are not cash; net receipts are not profit; an
Owner-declared perimeter cannot prove that omitted accounts do not exist.

Private rows and figures have short observations and hide when they expire or
you step away. Read them again with current authority. Publication consent does
not transfer to changed content or another destructive action. Withdrawal removes
eligibility; deletion erases local structured source content and retires dependent
evidence. Ordinary chat/provider prose has separate retention.

A failed refresh clears the previous private list or answer. A review that
changes or expires must be opened and acknowledged again; refreshing its rows
does not extend the original publication window. **Check figures** keeps the
last submitted type, period and selections until you choose **Show figures**
again. Editing the form alone does not change the answer being refreshed.

A write may commit before its final response reports a conflict or becomes
unavailable. **Not confirmed yet** keeps the original content-free operation
reference. Use **Check again** or the exact in-memory retry. No completed receipt
observed is not proof that a late commit cannot occur; do not add a replacement
operation. Reload recovery checks the original identity and never reconstructs a
financial command from newly entered form values. Actual-model 50-case
qualification, provider activation and hosted acceptance remain separate.

## Scoped Manager guidance

The Development backend also provides a separate guided Manager API for an authorized location and week: four scheduling questions and a weekly summary. Its UI is owned by Claude and depends on the selected release and feature settings. It does not give Managers Owner business records, payroll, knowledge administration, schedule mutation or free-text paid AI. Use only destinations actually offered to your signed-in role.
