# Fresh builder check: business evidence proposal

A fresh read-only backend builder returned four planning questions:

1. Can January and February both remain eligible, and what does correcting January replace?
2. Which source map prevents POS/accounting duplication or missing company coverage?
3. Can shared bank cash be attributed to one location?
4. Can a valid bank reconciliation discrepancy be published for Rondo to report?

The design now explicitly proposes: active nonoverlapping source/window versions
with exact-window correction; Owner-reviewed primary/corroborating coverage maps
and perimeter qualifications; no inferred shared-account allocation; valid
discrepancies publishable as findings, but no verified-cash/scenario claims from
unreconciled windows. These are Codex proposals for approval, not implemented
features or earlier Owner policy. No repeat reviewer was dispatched.
