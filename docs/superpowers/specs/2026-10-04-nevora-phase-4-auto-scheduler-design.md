## Alternatives and chosen planner

A first-valid greedy loop is rejected: a flexible worker can consume a scarce position/Lead/location opportunity and leave an avoidable gap. A heavy external solver is unnecessary and would require separate approval. Choose pure TypeScript constrained-first deterministic search with bounded backtracking, a best-valid incumbent, employee-specific validation caching and a bounded improvement pass. The engine returns diagnostics, not an unsupported optimality promise.

The planner receives an immutable scheduling snapshot; it has no React, database, provider, clock timer or machine-zone dependency. It must not mutate caller input. Dates and timing are explicit. Same semantic inputs/week/limits produce the same assignments, gaps and warnings, independent of input-array order. Wall-clock timing is recorded outside the planner and never determines a result.

Same-family/key overlapping claims cannot double reserve capacity; equivalent adjacent claims coalesce. Enforce at most 256 entries and 64 KiB JSON with explicit failure, never truncation. GENERATED requires nonempty validated claims; MANAGER/legacy rows require none. The containing workspace/timezone/week/location participates in the need key. No employee, fairness rank, generated shift ID, name, row order or raw window ID defines scheduling-need identity.

Derive maximal normalized need runs PER family from shared configured coverage: POSITION takes that position's maximum applicable quota; STAFFING is extra configured person capacity beyond the sum of independent named quotas; LEAD takes required Lead periods. Keys include that family's relevant configured run, identity and quantity. STAFFING also stores a bounded canonical basis of configured staffing minimum and sorted position-ID/quantity facts determining its residual capacity, and includes that basis in deterministic need identity: coincidentally equal residual quantities from materially different configurations are insufficient identity. Canonical ISO civil-minute strings and server SHA-256 over the explicit version/workspace/timezone/week/location/family/position/run/capacity/basis are acceptable; no machine-timezone conversion. Cosmetic names/window splitting/order do not expire an exclusion; materially changed time/location/position/capacity does. A required-position Lead stores POSITION and LEAD claims for the SAME person. Lead is never additional headcount. A necessary Lead-only extra has no fake position/staffing claim.
