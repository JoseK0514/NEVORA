Add to your calendar app

Apple CalendariPhone, iPad and Mac
  1. Copy your private link from NEVORA.
  2. On iPhone or iPad, open Calendar, then Calendars and Add Calendar. Choose Add Subscription Calendar.
  3. Paste the link and follow the setup prompts. On Mac, choose File, then New Calendar Subscription.
Apple setup guide (opens a new tab)
Google CalendarComputer required for setup
  1. Copy your private link from NEVORA.
  2. On a computer, open Google Calendar. Beside Other calendars, choose Add other calendars, then From URL.
  3. Paste the link and choose Add calendar.

The mobile Google Calendar app cannot add a URL subscription. Your link allows access without signing in to NEVORA; keep it private.

Google setup guide (opens a new tab)
OutlookSet up on the web
  1. Copy your private link from NEVORA.
  2. In Outlook on the web, open Calendar, then Add calendar and Subscribe from web.
  3. Paste the link and choose Import.

Use a subscription to receive updates. Uploading a calendar file does not keep it up to date.

Outlook setup guide (opens a new tab)

Calendar apps refresh on their own schedule. Check NEVORA for the latest schedule. Creating or copying a link does not confirm a calendar connection.
