Subscribe in your calendar app

Apple CalendariPhone, iPad, and Mac
  1. Copy your private calendar link.
  2. On iPhone or iPad, open Calendar, then Calendars and Add Calendar. Choose Add Subscription Calendar.
  3. Paste the link and follow the prompts. On Mac, choose File, then New Calendar Subscription.
Apple subscription guide
Google CalendarSet up on a computer
  1. Copy your private calendar link.
  2. On a computer, open Google Calendar. Next to Other calendars, choose Add other calendars, then From URL.
  3. Paste the link and choose Add calendar.
Google subscription guide
OutlookSet up on the web
  1. Copy your private calendar link.
  2. In Outlook on the web, open Calendar. Choose Add calendar, then Subscribe from web.
  3. Paste the link and complete the import.
Outlook subscription guide
