# Owner advisor primary-source research

The read-only `legal_research` tool and **Primary legal sources** panel accept only explicit `PR_US` or `US_FEDERAL` jurisdiction and `working_time`, `overtime_classification` or `source_status`. Timezone never selects jurisdiction. PR working-time research includes a relevant federal source; inspect federal classification separately. Collections do not determine coverage of a worker or employer.

## Trust and authority

Owner/company/session rechecks surround every retrieval. Results share the advisor's finite private display and authority-retirement lifecycle. There is no new endpoint, public cache, migration, rule-state write or background process. Inspection needs no model connection. Model calls use the existing eight-slot/six-round budget; two decision-review attempts remain the maximum.

`legal-sources.ts` is the exact source identity/URL registry. Arguments cannot supply URLs, queries, worker facts, credentials or referrers. GET requests are HTTPS, credentialless, no-store and redirect-error, with only a fixed Accept header. Limits: three sources, eight seconds each including streaming, 256 KiB per source. Hashes cover complete received bytes, not text projection. Failed/oversize/redirected responses have no verified hash or text. No company facts go to public sites. Unavailable sources stay unavailable; no silent mirror or fallback.

HTML becomes lossy plain text without executing scripts or retrieving embedded resources. Remaining text is untrusted, including embedded instructions. Extracts cap at 4,000 characters and disclose truncation; the complete `LegalResearch` JSON projection caps at 24,000 characters. Historical preambles can precede operative amendments: incomplete extracts cannot establish current thresholds. PDFs are fetched/hashed but **unread, metadata-only**. Hashes establish byte identity, not current consolidated law or interpretation. Effective intervals remain unknown in shipped results.

The DTRH Law 41 notice reports an annulled subject; the notice itself has unreviewed document status. The fixed May 15, 2026 GPO amendment is a dated publication discussing vacated provisions, not an eCFR fallback or proof of later developments. eCFR redirects remain refused.

Ordinary citations retain the internal-route allowlist. External URLs appear only in validated typed cards with `noreferrer noopener`. Legal reads return zero generic citations, preserving `ADVISOR_MAX_ANSWER_SOURCES = 15`; typed cards allow three. Models cannot mint cards or upgrade readiness.

## Qualified review and applicability

`QUALIFIED_LEGAL_REVIEWS` defaults to **empty**. No reviewer, qualification, interpretation or applicability is certified. Owner assertions, stored company-rule approval and memory cannot certify qualification. `legalApplicability` is a pure gate for a later deliberately integrated assessment. Current research has no confirmed case facts and always returns `readiness: unverified`, `financialClearance: false`.

1. A qualified reviewer independently checks the complete official document, jurisdiction, amendments, judicial status and later developments. Record identity and qualification; Owner attestation is insufficient.
2. Bind a version-controlled entry to exact `sourceId`, `sourceVersion`, full-byte SHA-256 and a separate `interpretationVersion`. Record reviewer, qualification, review/expiry dates, inclusive effective interval, jurisdiction/topic, test-vector references and exact required fact/value predicates. A truncated extract is not a complete reviewed source.
3. Add independently derived positive/negative vectors covering date boundaries, jurisdiction, factual conflicts, hash/version changes, expiry, annulment, supersession and unread/unavailable sources. Record reviewer approval of vectors. A `reviewed_match` concerns only those facts and interpretation; financial clearance remains false.
4. Separately implement and review actual-fact provenance and qualified-review presentation. The shipped research UI intentionally admits only unreviewed/hash-changed cards, unknown effective dates and no reviewer: adding an entry alone cannot enable legal clearance. No model assertion fills missing facts; contradictory confirmed facts fail closed. Private fact evidence never enters source requests.
5. Rerun source tests, authority integration, public parser and browser acceptance. Legal-rule changes, payroll calculations and production approval remain separate governed work.

Hash/version changes quarantine old reviews. Unavailable, annulled, superseded, expired, jurisdiction-mismatched or out-of-interval sources fail the gate. Revoke/remove acceptance through version-controlled review with historical receipts retained in approved records. Rollback disables the inspection/tool path or restores the previous application commit; no business or rule-state writes require reversal. Never restore an old hash to conceal changed law. Observation timestamps are not legal freshness.

## Verification boundaries

Offline tests cover exact requests, MIME/redirect rejection, byte/time limits, cancellation, HTML injection, PDFs, truncation and hashes. Pure tests cover reviewed identity, missing/conflicting facts and status/date invalidation. Owned fictional SQL tests exercise Owner checks around retrieval. Public receipts establish availability/hash only. SSR tests are not browser acceptance; Task 5 covers desktop/mobile/keyboard, loading/failure and authority retirement. No paid provider inference or legal correctness certification is implied.
