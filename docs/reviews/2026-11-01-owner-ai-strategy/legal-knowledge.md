**Direct assessment.** The existing chat does **not have a verified capability to “understand laws” at the requested level**. It has an operations reasoning prompt, bounded company reads, application scheduling checks, historical payroll exception metadata, and some separately implemented payroll rules with provenance. The model may have general legal information from training; this inspection does not establish its accuracy or currency. There is no legal-source retrieval, jurisdiction resolution, legal applicability assessment, conflict resolution or legal claim validation tool in the inspected advisor. It cannot substantiate a broad legal-awareness claim.

Build the owner decision profile and constructive challenge/alternatives in the existing bounded operational scope, while legal readiness proceeds **in parallel**. Legal readiness is a prerequisite for any new claimed legal clearance or automated decision relying on wages, work/rest limits, leave, classification, deductions or worker rights. It must not become a deferred item that later recommendations silently assume. Existing configured-rule feasibility can still be described accurately as configured-rule feasibility, with actual availability and legal readiness outside that claim.

**Top three next steps.**

1. Establish a server-owned capability and applicability boundary: legal knowledge unavailable by default; explicit work jurisdiction, legal employer, event/pay date, relevant worker/program category and supporting facts before applying a legal rule. Owner preferences rank options inside constraints; they cannot supply missing facts, invent law or waive protected rights. Preserve useful generic operational options when legal applicability is unknown, but withhold the law-dependent clearance/action.
2. Create a small reviewed legal-source/rule registry for the first declared jurisdiction and supported topics. Retrieve approved primary sources with version/status/effective-date metadata; have a qualified reviewer approve interpretations and deterministic rule mappings. Reuse the existing payroll provenance and unknown/configured/not-applicable patterns, adding the legal provenance they lack.
3. Gate a limited legal-aware pilot on an independently reviewed bilingual adversarial benchmark, deterministic rule tests, source freshness/conflict checks, privacy review and explicit activation/budget approval. Do not market a general legal expert or compliance guarantee. No production authorization follows from this report.

**Recommended smallest reliable legal layer.**

Use a curated source registry plus reviewed deterministic rules, with retrieval for research and explanation. Start with a narrow, explicitly approved jurisdiction/topic inventory rather than open-ended “knows all laws.” A server-controlled `research_legal_sources` tool should accept a topic, explicit jurisdiction and relevant date/facts; fetch only approved public sources, with redirect/size/timeout/content-type limits. It must not send employee data to a public search service. Public-source content remains untrusted data and cannot change tools or rules.

Each source record needs authority/instrument type, issuer, canonical URL, section/page, original language, content hash, publication/revision date, effective interval, repeal/annulment/injunction/supersession status and relationships, fetched time, last legal review, reviewer and permitted use. Preserve historical versions. Maintain separately the date an event is governed and the date the system learned a change; court changes can have retrospective consequences. A later timestamp is not necessarily a higher authority.

Each executable rule needs supported jurisdictions/topics/categories, explicit applicability predicates and required evidence, exceptions, controlling source references, reviewed interpretation/version, effective interval, accepted unresolved limits, deterministic test vectors and a kill switch. Separate legal requirements, contractual entitlements, company policy and owner preferences in both data and wording. Never let a model rewrite a legal rule, convert `UNKNOWN` into `NOT_APPLICABLE`, or accept an Owner instruction as evidence of statutory permission.

Return typed evidence distinguishing: observed application facts; a current reviewed rule matched to verified facts; unreviewed legal research; and blocked applicability/conflict/staleness. Avoid model-generated confidence percentages. Every legal proposition needs a supporting section-level citation and qualification; the server should render status, dates, jurisdiction and verified rule identity. An evidence-ID check alone is insufficient: verify source support and applicability independently. A prompt or keyword detector can assist routing but cannot be the sole enforcement of this boundary.

If sources disagree, an effective-date interval is missing, a page changes materially, the court/regulator status is uncertain, a translation differs, or facts leave the supported scope, suspend the affected legal conclusion/rule approval and show the conflict. Retrieve governing material and escalate to the designated qualified legal/payroll reviewer; retain safe generic operational options. Do not silently adopt the more convenient, newer-looking or cheaper interpretation, and do not overwrite historical approved payroll results.

For constructive challenge, make the assistant identify the business goal, the protected constraint, the decision-changing missing fact and two feasible alternatives. Examples to investigate include reallocating qualified coverage, voluntarily changing timing through the existing consent workflow, reducing service scope/opening time, or building a qualified backup pool. Their feasibility must come from actual tools and approved rules; the assistant cannot promise savings, availability, legal compliance or new authority.

Before enabling a legal-aware pilot, use at least **60 independently authored scenarios**, balanced English/Spanish and including every case above, missing-fact variants, effective-date edges and competing-authority cases. Record exact model/config/source/rule versions and run each high-risk case at least three times using an explicitly approved spend cap. Require **zero critical failures**: fabricated/unsupported legal clearance, worker-rights override, wrong jurisdiction/date applied as fact, obsolete law applied without qualification, privacy leak or unauthorized action. Require 100% section-level citation validity and 100% correct abstention/escalation on designated unknown/conflict/stale cases; target at least 95% independently scored support/applicability accuracy on answerable in-scope questions. An unsupported legal conclusion is a failure even if it happens to sound correct.

Independently test deterministic rules against reviewed expected results at every threshold/date/category boundary; require all required vectors to pass. Demonstrate source change → quarantine → reviewer approval → versioned activation, plus outage/fallback, revocation and rollback behavior. Reviewers must assess source support and applicability, not just fluency. Any critical failure blocks the legal capability, while a bounded operations assistant may retain an explicit legal-unavailable scope. Passing this pilot is not blanket legal accuracy or production certification; preserve NEVORA’s separate release and independent-review gates.
