### Early clock-in grace

- **Setting:** Settings → Clocker → Shift matching, "Early clock-in grace",
  whole minutes 0–30, Owner only, English and Spanish copy. Stored in
  `clocker_schedule_match_policy` (one row per workspace; no row means 0) with
  append-only `clocker_schedule_match_policy_revisions` (actor, time, previous
  and new value). Triggers require the actor to be an active Owner, enforce
  version 1 then +1, and block delete/truncate.
- **Matching:** a shift covers `[start − G, end)`. G = 0 produces exactly the
  previous context. A punch inside another shift or inside two grace windows is
  `AMBIGUOUS (OVERLAPPING_SHIFTS)`; an unresolved local-time shift stays
  `AMBIGUOUS (UNRESOLVED_LOCAL_TIME)`. Recorded and paid time never move.
- **Evidence:** the punch reads the saved grace on the server once per clock-in.
  Missing row, failed read or a database without 0069 all mean 0, never a
  blocked punch. The captured context stays `version: 1` with an optional
  `earlyGraceMinutes` only when G > 0. `clocker_resolve_schedule_context` is
  replaced with the same signature and refuses a grace that differs from the
  saved setting (existing same-command `SCHEDULE_CHANGED` retry). History checks
  a match against the grace recorded with the punch, not today's setting.
- **0069 grants** the two tables to an existing isolated APP role, as 0061 does.
- **Builder evidence (disposable loopback PostgreSQL 16, fictional data):**
  `vitest.clocker-c6-integration` 44 passed, `vitest.clocker-integration` 709
  passed, `vitest.release1-migrations` 11 passed; real-browser pass at
  320/390/768/1440 px with keyboard, reduced motion, stale-save, read-failure
  and Manager-closed checks. Screen reader not tested.

### Long-open punch flag

- The attendance engine already raised `OPEN_AFTER_SCHEDULE_END` for an open
  entry matched to a shift whose end had passed. The gap from the Nov 14 review
  was an open punch with **no** single matching shift (e.g. an 11:24 PM
  clock-in), which never got a duration flag.
- New observation `OPEN_PAST_LIMIT` ("No clock-out recorded · open more than 12
  hours.", label "No clock-out after 12 hours", tone critical) for a consistent
  open entry whose schedule is unmatched, ambiguous or not evaluated, once open
  strictly more than `OPEN_ENTRY_REVIEW_LIMIT_HOURS = 12`. Exactly 12:00:00 is
  not flagged; a matched entry never gets both flags.
- Review only: nothing writes to punches, timecards or corrections; recorded,
  timecard, payroll and export totals are unchanged. It flows into the Action
  Center and period-report notes through existing paths.
- `engineVersion` stays 1: projections are computed per read and never stored,
  existing kinds and IDs keep their meaning, and an old browser tab fails closed
  to "Attendance unavailable" until reloaded.
- A complete `ALL_OBSERVATION_KINDS` fixture makes any future kind fail typecheck
  until every consumer lists it.
