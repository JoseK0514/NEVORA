## Local fixture entry points

- `?page=notifications`: own inbox. Add `notifications=callout`, `unavailable`, `partial`, `stale`, `denied` or `signed-out` for explicit source/read fixtures. `marketplace=off` withholds exchange/callout alerts.
- `?page=updates`: permitted workplace messages. Add `updates=off`, `denied`, `signed-out`, `no-assignment`, `overnight-team`, `unavailable`, `partial`, `stale`, `audience-changed` or `uncertain` for the named local state.
- `?page=updates&update=opening-update`: current required update. Combine with `updates=expired` or `withdrawn` to verify removed content, `stale` to verify revised-content review, or `uncertain` to verify retained acknowledgement recovery.
- Existing `state=loading`, `error`, `empty` and `partial` apply to the shell. These are fictional demonstration inputs, not account impersonation or real workplace settings.
