Changes next to authentication, for your review

Session end. src/lib/auth/session.ts now redirects to /login?ended=1, with returnTo when the same-origin Referer is a portal page that safeReturnTo accepts. When and how a session ends is unchanged.
Wrong-role visits. requireRoles in src/lib/auth/permissions.ts redirects to the new /app/restricted page in place of /app. Authorization is unchanged. Four guards in src/app/app/schedule/settings/actions.ts (lines 33, 125, 179, 216) still call redirect('/app') directly.
Language. src/lib/auth-lang.server.ts reads Accept-Language when no nv-lang cookie exists.
