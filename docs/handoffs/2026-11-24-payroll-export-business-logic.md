## BL-17 and BL-23

**BL-17.** Source review found that provider-import approval and manual publication used existing authority and revision checks without checking the actor's own money row. The server now refuses both actions with `PAYROLL_SELF_REVIEW` (HTTP 409) when the actor has nonzero gross, net, or employer contributions on the revision. Complete-roster zero-dollar placeholders remain eligible for review. A refused approval preserves `REVIEW_REQUIRED`. A refused publish preserves `APPROVED` and creates no publication job or operation receipt. Successful exact replay returns the original receipt. Manual and queued publication of a historical self-approved provider revision are held with `INDEPENDENT_PAYROLL_APPROVAL_REQUIRED`. A queued refusal is `FAILED` with that exact job error. Existing approved records and published artifacts retain their history.

The reviewer must be an active Owner or a currently authorized Manager with the required payroll approval and full object/location coverage, and must have no financial interest in the run. If the pilot has no eligible reviewer, Jose needs to choose between assigning an already-supported independent approver and holding NEVORA statement release for that period while the business uses its external payroll process. The first customer's reviewer roster remains unverified.

**BL-23.** The existing P4 integration assertion accepts reopening after a draft child exists. Server inspection confirms that this exception is shared by the provider statement path and is based on a child-run link, independent of approval status or employee-level intent. The payroll review verified a covered paid employee stays blocked when the lock applies, an unrelated employee outside the approved roster and a disjoint period can reopen, and the existing completed-operation replay still returns the original receipt without a second write. The shared regression and new draft-lock behavior belong to Chat 1. [Chat 1 dependency](https://thevelani-team.atlassian.net/browse/SCRUM-197?focusedCommentId=10646)

The launch contract is to retain the existing `409 {status:'blocked',reason:'payroll_approved'}` on a protected new reopen while a correction is only `REVIEW_REQUIRED`. Derive coverage from the governing approved snapshot and the exact employee and period. Provider imports retain full-roster zero rows, so decide payable source coverage explicitly using the approved population and evidence. Any later exception requires an independently authorized intent bound to workspace, original approved run ID and exact revision, employee ID, exact local timecard period, reason, and current finalized timecard revision and source hash. Validate against every applicable approved revision and consume the intent in the same transaction as reopen. Bind it to the actor and the normalized command. A completed identical replay requires current authority and returns the original receipt. A changed replay conflicts. Keep the approved run, snapshot and issued documents immutable. Payroll owns any future issuance contract once its business authorization and lifecycle dependency are agreed.

## Export and statements review

| Finding | Disposition |
| --- | --- |
| BL-01 · Multiple pay rates within one shift | Source entries retain scheduled position and location provenance. Paid export has no record of an actual intra-shift duty/rate change. A customer with intra-shift changes needs an operator-reviewed provider allocation backed by their records. Capturing rate segments and deriving amounts remain scope decisions. |
| BL-14 · Overnight and period boundaries | The paid CSV splits by the workspace's local civil date, with half-open pay-period boundaries. A fictional Puerto Rico overnight shift with a meal spanning midnight exported 0.5 and 1.5 paid hours across adjacent dates. The paid rest counted as paid. A repeated midnight in `America/Havana` returned zero on the baseline for a 30-minute shift because PostgreSQL selected the later midnight while Temporal chose the earlier one. New exports now refuse when those period edges disagree. Shared timecard source normalization and re-review of affected finalized sources are required before such windows can export. Existing receipts remain recoverable as issued. |
| BL-22 · Period-wide final lock | Each finalized card and manifest is immutable. A handoff is a report tied to specific source, population revision and artifact hashes. Later changes can make `current:false`; a new handoff from that stale manifest conflicts. A whole-period operational seal, who could lift it, and post-submission correction policy require a product decision. First-customer practice is to re-review drift and reconcile any prior provider import before uploading again. |
| BL-24 · Overtime reconciliation | Reviewed export exposes exact elapsed, paid, unpaid-meal and paid-rest quantities. It has no regular, daily/weekly overtime or premium classification. For the first customer, compare the provider's regular/overtime/premium totals against the approved workweek, overnight attribution and reviewed source. Record how double-counting and threshold crossings are handled. If the provider requires preclassified or multi-rate rows, agree on a reviewed operator workflow or hold that use case. Native statutory validation remains [SCRUM-51](https://thevelani-team.atlassian.net/browse/SCRUM-51). |
| BL-25 · Effective-dated provider mappings | A saved profile is versioned and explicitly selected. Matching employee IDs, required columns, and reviewed precision are checked. A profile has no effective-date selector or per-site/position earnings/rate routing. Freeze the exact customer account mapping revision before generation and record its approved applicability. Account acceptance remains [SCRUM-49](https://thevelani-team.atlassian.net/browse/SCRUM-49). |
| BL-26 · Receipt handoff | Personal receipt download is actor-scoped. A current Company successor can see period receipt references and prepare a company manifest using the original preparer's receipt when source, population and profile still match. Once a manifest exists, it can serve the same bytes under current Company authority. If the preparer leaves before a manifest and their departure or other changes cause source drift, re-review the population and original receipt eligibility. Escalate unreconciled stale artifacts through the payroll operator. |

Keep provider evidence distinct from that reported state. Recover uncertain submissions with the original operation/body or stored artifact, and reconcile the provider reference before any new submission.

## UI contract

- `POST /api/payroll/runs` HTTP 409 `error:'PAYROLL_SELF_REVIEW'`. Keep the current review or approved state, identify the need for another authorized reviewer, and offer the normal route to hand over review. Keep zero-amount roster placeholders distinct from financially interested actors.
- HTTP 409 `error:'INDEPENDENT_PAYROLL_APPROVAL_REQUIRED'` and a publish job with `status:'FAILED'` and `error:'INDEPENDENT_PAYROLL_APPROVAL_REQUIRED'` mean publication is held for independent payroll resolution. Keep published status and document availability server-driven. Suppress the ordinary retry affordance for this job error.
- `POST /api/payroll-export` HTTP 400 `{state:'invalid',reason:'PERIOD_BOUNDARY_MISMATCH'}` means the server refused a fresh file for the selected period. Add it to the deterministic refusal list. Show the period and business zone and direct the operator to time-record reconciliation. The intentional export period and hours need renewed review before any replacement file. For ordinary overnight exports, label paid rows by local business date and keep paid hours distinct from Recorded Time.
- When Chat 1's shared fix lands, `POST /api/manager/timecards` HTTP 409 `{status:'blocked',reason:'payroll_approved'}` keeps the card `FINALIZED` while a correction draft is pending. Explain the approved-payroll lock without suggesting that opening a draft enables reopen.
- A period manifest with `current:false` is historical evidence. `POST /api/period-close` returns HTTP 409 `{state:'conflict'}` for a fresh handoff based on stale evidence. Historical artifact download uses the original company manifest and keeps the exact CSV. Show handoff events as reported states.
