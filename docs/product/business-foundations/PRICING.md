# Pricing decision — options for Jose

October 21, 2026 · Nothing here is public or final; the landing page shows no prices on purpose.

## What the category charges (for orientation)

Scheduling/time tools for SMBs price **per active employee per month**, usually with a flat location or
base fee, roughly: Homebase ~$20–80/location/mo (tiers), Connecteam ~$29–99/mo for the first 30 users,
7shifts ~$3–4/user/mo on mid tiers, When I Work ~$2.50–5/user/mo. (2025–26 list prices; re-verify before
publishing anything.)

## The decision, in three parts

1. **Unit.** Recommended: **per active employee per month**, counted the way NEVORA already counts
   active people in Team. It scales with value, and the Owner can predict it. A flat per-location fee is
   the alternative if PR owners react badly to per-head pricing.
2. **Number.** Pick a wedge price below the US tools' effective cost for a 10–25 person shop, since the
   pitch is "built for Puerto Rico" not "cheapest". A workable opening position: **$3/active
   employee/mo, $45/mo minimum per workspace**, founding customers locked for 12 months. Decide; don't
   publish until at least two real workspaces pay it.
3. **Collection in PR.** Customer #1–5: **manual invoice** (email, ACH/ATH Móvil Business/check) — no
   billing code needed. Card self-serve (Stripe) only once there's inbound demand the landing page
   generates. ATH Móvil matters: local SMBs pay with it daily.

## What's in the launch tier

Everything the app does today (scheduling, Inbox, Clocker time, timecards, pay prep and exports, Updates,
Insights). Don't tier features at launch — tiering needs data you don't have yet. Revisit after ~10
workspaces.

## What this blocks until decided

The pitch and the landing page's call-to-action copy ("Talk to us" works now; a price makes it convert),
the invoice template, and any billing/plan field Codex would add to the workspace model.
