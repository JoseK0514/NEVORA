Tablets (768–1023px), Direction B: two options

Today a tablet in portrait gets the older corporate layout: the 72px icon rail, a plain white top bar and flat white cards. Direction B starts at 1024 for desktop and below 768 for phones. These frames show the two ways to bring tablets into Direction B, page by page, for Owner and Manager, Employee and Lead. Fictional data: Northside Café & Bakery, Saturday, Nov 7.

Frames: iPad portrait 820×1180 and 768×1024 (iPad mini and older iPads). iPad landscape (1180) is already desktop and appears once at the end, with phone landscape. Nothing here is built yet.

Option A Big phone

The phone Direction B, centred: a teal hero, cards up to 640px (two columns where a page has several cards), the frosted tab bar at the bottom, and phone sheets and forms.

Option B Small desktop

The desktop Direction B that 1024 already shows, from 768 up: a collapsed 72px icon rail (48px touch targets), a teal top bar and band, cards lifting over the band, and desktop tables and the week grid.

Recommendation: Option B, small desktop

One layout per device. An iPad in landscape (1180) is already desktop B. With A, turning the same iPad would swap a bottom tab bar for a side rail and move every control. With B, rotating changes only the width.

Tablets are for the desk work. Owners and managers use a tablet at the counter for the week grid and timecards. B keeps the 7-day grid and the timecards table. A falls back to the phone's one-day view and leaves about 90px of empty canvas each side of a 640px column.

Touch. On a tablet the rail buttons grow from 40px to 48px. Top bar controls, buttons and fields are 44–52px. The rail sits under the left thumb when you hold the iPad. A bottom tab bar is easy to reach on a phone, but in the middle of an 820px screen it is no easier to reach than the rail.

Less to build. owner-b-desktop.css is one @media (min-width: 1024px) layer. B lowers it to 768 and adds a short tablet block: Home stacks Needs you, the Timecard panel overlays the list instead of docking, the grid uses compact times, and the rail uses 48px buttons. The shell already opens tablets as a rail (app-shell.tsx, TABLET_SIDEBAR_QUERY, 768–1279). A would have to move the phone shell's 767px switch point (the mobile header, the tab bar, and about a hundred phone rules across several CSS layers), then add centring and two-column rules, and give every desktop-only view (docked panels, tables, the grid) a phone version at tablet widths.

Open questions for Jose. 1) Employee desktop B isn't built yet, so with B, Employee and Lead tablets move together with Employee desktop. A could ship for employees sooner, because the phone B is built. Would you rather use A for Employee and Lead only, and B for Owner and Manager? (I'd keep B everywhere for one rule.) 2) At 768 the grid's day columns are 71px. Is a two-line time like "1:30p / 9:30p" acceptable, or should Schedule open in Day view under 900px?
