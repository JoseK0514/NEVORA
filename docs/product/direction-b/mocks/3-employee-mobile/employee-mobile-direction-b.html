Every Employee phone page in the look you approved: the brand teal hero, live figures on glass, white cards lifting over it, the frosted tab bar. Shown as Ana Colón on fictional data (Thursday, Nov 5). The five tab screens are the ones you approved before, polished with the rest.

Each page has a Today switch to compare with the current page. Nothing is built yet; this is the mock to approve first.

  • Tabs: Home, Schedule, Time, Pay, RequestsThe same five tabs a regular employee has today.
  • Pushed pagesA frosted back circle with the parent's name, the title on teal, and cards under it. The parent tab stays highlighted.
  • FormsOne white card with clear fields and one full-width main button that wakes up once the form is filled. Cancel is an X, with no bell or avatar.
  • Calm by defaultPhosphor icons, the app’s own set, in grey to say what a thing is. Colour lives only in the chips that give a status. No icon repeated down a list, and no titles that say the same thing twice.
  • Same featuresEvery page keeps what it has today. Pay shows amounts only where a statement exists.
