# 24 Accessibility Engineer (screen readers): semantics in the Owner and Manager app

**Verdict.** The building blocks are careful: real tables with `scope`, kit components that hide decoration, spoken labels for the roster blocks and week strip, native `<dialog>` sheets, and one deliberate live line on Home. The gaps are structural and won't show up in screenshots. Moving between tabs is silent because every page has the same document title. Timecards rebuilds its whole DOM every 30 seconds and after every approval, which throws away screen-reader focus. Several rows use an `aria-label` that drops the words people can see, which fails WCAG 2.5.3 and hides who and where from VoiceOver and TalkBack users.

## Recommendations

1. **Give every page its own document title, such as "Inbox · NEVORA" or "Timecards · NEVORA".**
   *Why:* only `src/app/layout.tsx:10` sets a title ("NEVORA Workforce"), and no page under `src/app/app/**` exports `metadata` or renders `<title>`. Next 16's route announcer speaks only when `document.title` changes (`node_modules/next/dist/client/components/app-router-announcer.js:52-63`). The shell doesn't move focus on navigation, so focus stays on the tab-bar link. A VoiceOver or TalkBack user who taps Schedule, Inbox or Timecards hears nothing and has to go looking for the new h1. It also fails WCAG 2.4.2 Page Titled, because every browser tab and app-switcher card reads the same, and Home's failure page (`src/app/app/page.tsx:68`) gets no announcement either. Use a `title.template` in `src/app/app/layout.tsx` plus `metadata`/`generateMetadata` per page, or React 19 `<title>` inside the client pages, for example "Inbox (10)".
   *Impact:* High. *Effort:* S. *Owner:* UI.

2. **Stop Timecards from unmounting its list, table and sheet body on every re-read.**
   *Why:* the 30-second poll calls `sync()`, then `clear()` and `refresh()` (`src/components/timecards.tsx:146-151`), which run `setData(null)` and `clearBatch()` (`:121-127`). The table and phone rows unmount (`:332`, `:356`), the open sheet loses `openItem` and its body goes blank (`:229`, `:385`), and the h2 falls back to "Timecard". The same happens after Approve, Finalize and Reopen (`:177`). Screen-reader and keyboard focus on a checkbox, a person button or the sheet's Approve button falls to `<body>` every 30 seconds. Ticked checkboxes are also cleared every 30 seconds (`clearBatch` → `setSelected([])`), so a manager selecting slowly loses the selection. `HoldHeight` only holds the height; it doesn't keep the nodes. Keep the last snapshot on screen while the next read is in flight, with an `aria-busy` container and disabled actions, and replace rows in place by `employeeId`. Clear the selection only when the source revision actually changes.
   *Impact:* High. *Effort:* M. *Owner:* UI (the read-validity rule stays as it is).

3. **Remove the row `aria-label` overrides that replace visible text, and add only the missing context as `sr-only` text after it.**
   *Why:* the kit's `Row` puts `label` on the link (`src/components/kit/kit.tsx:205`), which replaces everything the row shows. Two kinds of failure follow:
   - **WCAG 2.5.3 Label in Name.** On Home, the visible "Not clocked in" row is named "6 shifts with no clock‑in, open in Inbox", and the visible "Time‑off request" row is named "1 request waiting, open in Inbox" (`src/components/premium-home.tsx:174-185`, `QAOWNER-home-390.png`). In Inbox Plan ahead, the visible "People without hour targets", "Timecards to review" and "Time corrections" keep the label built for the original item (`src/components/manager-inbox.tsx:217-221`, `:133`). Voice Control ("tap Not clocked in") and Voice Access fail on these.
   - **Information loss.** The aria-label drops the visible meta: the per-location split ("Main Office 4 · West 2"), the requester ("Ana Rivera, time off"), and "Started 9:00 AM · Main Office" with how late ("2h 15m") on Inbox shift rows (`manager-inbox.tsx:163`, where the trailing text is `aria-hidden` at `:70`).
   Let the content form the name (title, then meta) and append a hidden ", 6, opens Inbox". Where a label is unavoidable, start it with the visible title.
   *Impact:* High. *Effort:* S. *Owner:* UI.

4. **Make Timecards' result messages reachable while the sheet is open.**
   *Why:* the sheet is a modal `<dialog>`, so the footer status (`timecards.tsx:375`) is inert behind it and its announcements are dropped. The sheet's own polite region exists only while `busy||pending` (`:411`). It's inserted already holding "Saving timecard action…", which most screen readers don't announce, and it's removed before the final `setMessage(feedback)` in `act()` (`:177`). After Approve, the last thing heard is "Checking period timecards…", not "Timecard approved." Keep one live region in the sheet mounted for as long as the sheet is open. Announce the outcome after the re-read, and leave the poll's routine "N timecards in this period" out of the region; today it's spoken every 30 seconds through the footer status.
   *Impact:* High. *Effort:* S. *Owner:* UI.

5. **Fix the Inbox's live-region and filter semantics.**
   *Why:*
   - A `role="status"` span sits inside the Refresh button (`manager-inbox.tsx:458-462`). The button's name becomes "Refresh Refreshing the Inbox…", and a live region nested in the focused control gets announced twice or not at all depending on the screen reader. Move it beside the button.
   - The filter buttons are `aria-pressed` toggles with their counts `aria-hidden` (`:375`). VoiceOver says "Act now, toggle button" while sighted users see "Act now 6". Timecards already does this correctly with `aria-label="Need review, 3"` (`timecards.tsx:335`).
   - Choosing a filter replaces the sections without any announcement (`:276-281`). Add a polite "Showing Requests, 2".
   *Impact:* Medium. *Effort:* S. *Owner:* UI.

6. **Stop the desktop sidebar's group labels being h2s.**
   *Why:* `app-shell.tsx:230` renders "Plan", "People", "Time and pay", "Team tools" and "Workspace" as `<h2>` before the page's `<h1>` in DOM order. The heading rotor and the H key land on four or five navigation labels before "Today", and the outline begins h2, h2, h2, h2, h1. The phone More sheet already does it the right way: `role="group"` with an `aria-label`, and the visible label hidden (`:423-424`). Use the same pattern in the sidebar, or `aria-labelledby` on a `<p>`. Also give the `<aside class="sidebar">` (`:217`) a name, or make it a `div`: today it's an unnamed "complementary" landmark wrapped around the primary navigation.
   *Impact:* Medium. *Effort:* S. *Owner:* UI.

7. **Cut the repeated naming on cards: region, heading and list all say the same thing.**
   *Why:* every titled `Card` with an `id` becomes a named region (`kit.tsx:155`), its h2 carries the name again, and `Rows label=` names the list a third time (`premium-home.tsx:171-173`, `:226`, `:241`; `manager-weekly-roster.tsx:140`). VoiceOver reads "Needs you, region; heading level 2, Needs you, 4; list, Needs you, 2 items" for each card. Drop `label` from `Rows` when the card has a title, or point the list at the heading with `aria-labelledby`. Also, "Working now" and "Later today" have no `titleLabel` (`premium-home.tsx:222`, `:240`). The inline `.cm-count` span is concatenated straight onto the title, so the accessible name is "Working now2", and braille shows that too. Every other counted card passes `titleLabel`.
   *Impact:* Medium. *Effort:* S. *Owner:* UI.

8. **Timecards table: add row headers, and put each disabled checkbox's reason in readable text.**
   *Why:* in the desktop table the person's name is a `<td>` button (`timecards.tsx:347`). Moving across a row with VO+arrows reads "Recorded, 38h 30m" without saying whose. The Schedule grid already uses `<th scope="row">` (`manager-weekly-roster.tsx:160`). On phones, the reason a checkbox is disabled is only in a `title` on the `<label>` (`timecards.tsx:288`), which iOS VoiceOver and TalkBack don't read. Use `aria-describedby` pointing at visible or `sr-only` text. Optionally render recorded and break totals with the kit's `Duration` (`kit.tsx`), so "38h 30m" is read as "38 hours 30 minutes" rather than letters.
   *Impact:* Medium. *Effort:* S. *Owner:* UI.

9. **Route success messages that arrive with a redirect through the shell's toaster.**
   *Why:* Schedule renders "Shift updated.", "Shift deleted.", "Availability deadline updated." and the "published" panel as `role="status"` nodes that already contain their text when the page arrives (`src/app/app/schedule/page.tsx:370-386`). Live regions inserted with content are announced unreliably by VoiceOver, TalkBack and NVDA. The toaster region is always mounted (`toaster.tsx:85`) and already handles `decided=`. Also rename that region from "Notifications" to something like "Messages", so it isn't confused with the bell. The bell's own label, "Notifications, 3 unread in your recent inbox" (`notification-bell.tsx:32`), mixes "inbox" with the Inbox tab; "Notifications, 3 unread" is clearer.
   *Impact:* Medium. *Effort:* S. *Owner:* UI.

10. **Tidy the Inbox sheet and the Schedule block names.**
    *Why:*
    - On phones, `InboxDetail` renders twice: in the sheet and in the hidden side column (`manager-inbox.tsx:465-468`). That gives two `id="inbox-detail-title"`. `aria-labelledby` and `focus()` work only because the sheet comes first in the DOM. The dialog and its inner `<section>` are both labelled by that same heading, so it's read as "Ana Rivera, dialog … Ana Rivera, region". Render the pane only when `wide`, and drop the section's label inside the sheet.
    - `ItemSheet` relies on the browser to return focus to the row on close (`:516-529`). iOS Safari doesn't focus links on tap, so restore focus to `[data-inbox-key]` explicitly.
    - A draft roster block is read as "…, draft, Cashier, Draft" (`manager-weekly-roster.tsx:46`, `:60`), and its `title` tooltip repeats everything again as the description (`:53`).
    *Impact:* Low. *Effort:* S. *Owner:* UI.

## What's good
- The Schedule grid is a proper data table: an `sr-only` caption, `scope="col"` and `scope="row"`, a spoken label for each shift that names person, day, time, place and state, a week strip whose buttons say "Monday, 19, today, 5 shifts", and a Show unscheduled toggle with `aria-expanded` (`manager-weekly-roster.tsx`).
- The kit consistently treats decoration as decoration: avatars, tiles, chevrons, skeletons and the timeline bars are `aria-hidden`, and Money and Duration carry `sr-only` spoken forms.
- Live regions are mostly disciplined. Home has one sr-only status line in the band (`premium-home.tsx:138`), and the error boundaries use a single `role="alert"` with the buttons kept outside it.

## Not checked / limits
- Code review only; no browser use, as my brief required. Nothing was run under VoiceOver, TalkBack, NVDA or Voice Control. Announcement order and the "Working now2" concatenation are inferred from the accname rules and the CSS (`.cm-count` is inline at 390px).
- I didn't trace focus return from native `<dialog>` on iOS Safari or Android Chrome; that needs a device.
- I didn't review Team, Insights, Updates, Notifications, Payroll, Settings or the request review pages in depth, nor the CommandFinder combobox beyond a scan. Spanish `lang` switching is out of scope; `<html lang="en">` is fixed (`src/app/layout.tsx:25`).
- The 30-second Timecards teardown (recommendation 2) is read from code. A 60-second browser check with a checkbox ticked would confirm the lost selection and focus.
