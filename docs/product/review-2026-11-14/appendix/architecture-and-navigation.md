### A6. Core workflow trace (code and navigation)

1. Publish: Schedule › Build and publish (schedule/page.tsx:345,361) → the employee's Schedule and notifications. Connected.
2. Clock in: only at `/clocker/[location]`. Prerequisites live in three places: tablet enrolment (Settings › Clocker tablets), PINs (person page, Owner only, or Settings › Clocker PINs), and the deployment flag. Nothing on Home, Schedule or Team points an Owner to this; the Home setup card does not include it (owner-setup-card.tsx:51-55). A Manager cannot set a PIN.
3. Punch → Timecards (managers) and Time (employees); Home "Who's working". Connected.
4. Correction: employee Time › entry › request; manager Things to do, Timecards "Time tools", and the timecard sheet's "Review correction" (timecards.tsx:444). Connected.
5. Approval: Timecards "Approve N ready", then a second step, "Finalize period", per card or batch (timecards.tsx:467, 560-571).
6. Period close: there is no page with that name. Finalising happens on Timecards; the per-person include/exclude review ("population") is inside a collapsed section at the bottom of Time exports (payroll-export.tsx:236-239). See AR-4.
7. Pay: Payroll › Prepare › Review › Fix › History. Payroll's blockers link back to Timecards, Time exports and Payroll settings (payroll-runs.tsx:485-487). Timecards never links forward to Payroll; its tools card offers only Time exports and Time corrections (timecards.tsx:547-549, 621-622).
8. Statement: employee Pay tab; notifications deep-link with `?statement=` (my-pay/page.tsx:20).

Places where a user must already know where to go: Clocker set-up; team Availability; the payroll population review; billing; the Timecards → Payroll hand-off; the Owner's Things to do on a phone (no tab when Payroll is shown).

### Answers to the specific checks

Distinct in purpose. The known overlap is the count shown on the bell versus Things to do (NV-016, still "partly").
- `/app/clean-manager`: not a route. `/app/ai-manager`: Owner-only advisor behind a flag, reachable from the sidebar, More and a Home button.
- `/app/more`: a real page for every role; the phone avatar opens it; on desktop it lights Home.
- `/app/help` vs `/access-help` vs `/support`: signed-in help (same content as /support); signed-out sign-in help that exists only with mobile access on; public support page. Not duplicated in code (Help reuses the SUPPORT document).
- Dev and preview routes in production: closed by runtime checks (AR-22).
