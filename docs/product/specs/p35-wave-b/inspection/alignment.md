# Wave B inspection, round 1: alignment and geometry

October 19, 2026 · Inspector: alignment and geometry (prefix AL-B) · Preview from the newest `access.json` (fictional data) · Owner `QAOWNER`, Manager `AUDIT-M`, Lead `AUDIT-LEAD`, Employee `AUDIT-E`, payroll reviewer `payroll-qa-reviewer`.

## Verdict

The page frame is right on every Wave B page. Card edges sit at 16 px on every phone width, cards are 16 px apart, card text is 20 px in, card heads hold their baselines, and buttons are 52 px with a 12 px radius. The band lines up with the cards at every width, including the 840 px Update detail and Compose pages. The defects are in what's inside the cards and in the desktop layouts:

- **Insights tables break at 768 and in the half-width cards at 1024.** They scroll sideways, and names wrap one word per line.
- **Insights figures break.** Durations split across two lines in Period summary ("8h / 26m"), and the At a glance values sit 20 px apart in the same row.
- **Spacing and alignment don't follow the form pattern** in places. Some fields are 12 px apart instead of 16. The Remove label sits 8 px below its title. Checkboxes and icons centre on two-line labels instead of the first line.
- **Some desktop layouts look unfinished.** At 1024, Scheduling rules squeezes or stacks its fields, and the Insights grid leaves holes of up to 96 px.

One judgment needs the coordinator. Wave B fields are 52 px, but the shared rule (WR-55, PARALLEL.md) says 48 px. On the Payroll section the fields change size between the Payroll runs and Payroll settings tabs.

Count: **0 P0, 3 P1, 18 P2.**

**How I measured.** Playwright (Chromium; WebKit and Firefox spot checks), using the saved sessions. Box positions come from `getBoundingClientRect`. Baselines and cap-height centres come from Range client rects plus canvas font metrics. Every non-GET request was aborted, with one exception: Insights reads its data with `POST /api/reports`. That handler only calls `readReports`, and `src/lib/reports/service.ts` has no writes. I let that exact path through so the ready state could be measured, and kept `/api/reports/export` and everything else aborted. Nothing was saved, published or sent. All coordinates are CSS px in page space.

**Where the files are.** Crops are in `/private/tmp/claude-501/-Users-josek0514-Desktop-NEVORA-UI-READINESS--claude-worktrees-p35-wave-b-808dfd/55ac0f42-ec52-4b85-a911-9b8ee813b392/scratchpad/inspect/alignment/crops/` ("crops/" below). JSON dumps are in `out/` and scripts are in the same folder.
