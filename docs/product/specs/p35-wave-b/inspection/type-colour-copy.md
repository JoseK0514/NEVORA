## Verdict

**Not at zero. There are 0 P0, 5 P1 and 14 P2 findings in this area.**

- **What holds.** Colour holds everywhere:
  - The lowest text contrast on any Wave B page is 5.14:1 (muted on the inset box). The lowest meaningful icon is 3.04:1.
  - On phones, at default text size, no text is under the 15px floor.
  - Every Wave B element uses a kit type role, except the "No target" count.
  - Every error the pages render has an icon and a word, except one payroll line (TC-B-05).
  - `keepWhole` is used on every Insights, Templates, Scheduling rules, Update and Compose date I saw. Across 532 snapshots, no time or date splits on those five pages.
- **Where it breaks.** It breaks in three places:
  - **Large text.**
    - The Update detail's status pills and receipt heading are cut by the card from 125% at 320px.
    - Insights "At a glance" breaks its values mid-word ("availab/le", "Incom/plete") and cuts its labels at 150%.
    - At 1024px and 200% text, primary buttons are wider than their cards on Insights, Templates and Scheduling rules, so "Add coverage p…" is cut.
  - **Payroll settings dates.** They are formatted outside the kit helpers. "Effective Thu, Jan / 1" splits at 320px at default size and has no year. "Recorded 10/19/2026, 9:12:08 AM" is numeric, with seconds.
  - **Copy:**
    - The payroll pages show the browser's raw "Failed to fetch".
    - Compose and Update detail use straight apostrophes in 8 strings and show a raw time zone code ("America/Puerto Rico").
    - Insights, Scheduling rules, Compose and Update detail still carry internal language: "comparisons", "limitations", "sources", "context", "unconfigured", "Schedule Health", "coverage window", "Submission advance".

## How it was measured

- **Collector.** `collect.js` reads every visible text node inside `[data-nv-page]` with `getComputedStyle`:
  - size, line height, weight and family
  - colour, against the background composited through every ancestor
  - the WCAG ratio and the effective opacity
  - `Range.getClientRects()` on every time, date, "N of M" and "N unit" match, to find splits
  - clipping by any ancestor that hides overflow, line-clamp and ellipsis overflow
  - each field's border contrast, value, placeholder and select width
  - every icon's colour against its background
  - every alert, error, notice and `aria-invalid`
  - all visible text, placeholders, `aria-label`s and options, for the copy rules
- **Matrix (Chromium).** 472 page snapshots, with every `details` opened:
  - Owner `QAOWNER` and Manager `AUDIT-M`: Insights, Templates, Scheduling rules, Update detail, Compose and Payroll settings. Widths 320, 360, 390, 430, 844×390, 768, 1024 and 1440. Text at 100, 125, 150 and 200% on phone widths, 100 and 200% on desktop.
  - Lead `AUDIT-LEAD`, Employee `AUDIT-E` and long name `AUDIT-LONG`: Update detail and Compose. `AUDIT-E` also on Insights (no access). Widths 320, 390, 844×390 and 1440.
  - `payroll-qa-reviewer` on Payroll runs, and `payroll-qa-owner` on Payroll settings and runs, at the same four widths.
  - Variants:
    - Templates `?saved=1`, `?archived=1`, `?error=save`, `?applied=3&skipped=1&warnings=1` and `?week=2026-10-26`
    - Scheduling rules `?saved=1`, `?error=invalid_hours`, `?windowSaved=1`, `?error=invalid_staffing`, `?error=duplicate_window` and `?windowDeleted=1`
    - Owner at 320 and 1440, Manager at 390
- **Firefox and WebKit.** 50 snapshots: Owner on the six pages at 390 and 1440 (100 and 150%), and `AUDIT-E` on Update detail and Compose at 320 (100 and 125%). Plus reduced motion in Chromium at 390.
- **States (`states.mjs`, 390px).** 47 scenarios:
  - **Reads held, aborted or answered 401/403, and offline:** Insights, Update detail (Owner and Employee), Compose, Payroll settings and Payroll runs.
  - **Writes aborted or held:**
    - Insights CSV, Acknowledge, Withdraw, Preview audience, Save template, Save company preferences
    - Insights dates changed and invalid period
    - Compose empty and over-length title, expiration, Shift note, Important update, Today's team, edit, and the Lead compose
    - Scheduling rules "No one matches" and the Manager's read-only policy
    - No-access redirects
  - Payroll writes were blocked before reaching the server.
- **Retries.** Loads that hit the shared server's database timeouts (`canceling statement due to statement timeout`, 503) were retried once and passed. Nothing below comes from a failed load.
- **Source drift.** During the run the worktree gained commit `9c92fad` (landscape type) and uncommitted edits to `premium-wave-b.css`, `reports.tsx` and `communication-detail.tsx`. I re-took the crops below on the current preview, after those edits. Every P1 and P2 crop still shows the defect.
