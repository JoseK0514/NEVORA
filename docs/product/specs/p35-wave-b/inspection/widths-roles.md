## Verdict

**Not at zero.** Nothing scrolls the page sideways: 0 of 1,316 measurements, across 7 pages, 8 accounts, 8 viewports, 4 text sizes and 3 engines. Roles are routed correctly. The pages share the kit's band, card, title, gap and button geometry with Inbox, Schedule, Team and Time off, to the pixel. The defects sit inside cards, where fixed or rem-based widths meet narrow columns:

- **Insights tables don't fit the columns they switch into.** From 761px "Schedule vs actual" is 520px in a 453px box. At 1024 the half-width "Hours by location / position" tables are 397 and 413px in 350px boxes. In all three engines at 100% text, the last column ("Difference", "Breaks recorded") is hidden behind a sideways scroll inside the card.
- **Scheduling rules squeezes at 768–1440 with larger text.** The staffing field pair leaves "Designated lead required" cut at the card edge. At 1024 and 200% the 13rem side navigation (416px) is wider than the content (276px), and the fields overlap.
- **Fields and pills clip at default size.** On phones the update's status pill is cut at the card edge from 125% (320) or 150% (360/390). Weekly hours shows "Not …" at 360 (and at 390, 430 and 1440 in Firefox, which draws spin buttons inside the field). Payroll settings selects clip into their chevron at 1024.
- **Templates' "Apply to this week" collapses to one word per line**: 90×176px at 360 at 200%, beside a 186px Archive.

On a 1440 desktop at 100% the pages look finished. A demanding client would notice the uneven At a glance values on Insights, the Owner's "Settings" highlight on Scheduling rules, and the 52px fields beside 48px fields everywhere else.
