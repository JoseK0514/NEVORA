# Profile (Phase 3.5) inspection: interaction and states

Inspector: interaction and states (prefix IS). October 19, 2026. Round 1.

Scripts and screenshots: `/private/tmp/claude-501/-Users-josek0514-Desktop-NEVORA-UI-READINESS--claude-worktrees-p35-profile-6cdee8/e8429433-65b2-4ff4-835a-8f879fb2454f/scratchpad/inspect/interaction/` (screenshots in `shots/`). `lib.mjs` uses the harness's request handling plus three extra modes: a held save, a save that is slow and then aborted (`slowabort`), and a read mode that can change mid-test (for a 401 on the save followed by a 401 on the re-read). Every save was answered in the browser and nothing was saved on the server. Sign out was pressed only with every POST held in the browser and then aborted.

## Verdict

The read states, the guard against repeat taps and the pending looks are solid. Three, four or two quick taps, Space presses, Enter presses or clicks send exactly one save in Chromium, Firefox and WebKit. Every read state says what happened, and the 10-second give-up works. The weak point is what happens to focus and to the live regions while a control is busy. Every control that disables itself or disappears while it works drops keyboard focus to `<body>`: the alert switches, both Try again buttons, and Try again again every 30 s on its own. The Try again buttons' accessible names don't contain the words on screen. When the read changes underneath a save, the save message is announced as "Alert", is announced twice, or isn't announced at all. Smaller items: the offline state says nothing near the alert list, the offline save message can grow its row, the "Show names" button jumps 2,679 px below the screen, two focus rings are clipped by the card, and phones shift 0.034 when the read arrives. Totals: 3 P1, 9 P2.
