## Verdict

Four P2 findings, no P0 or P1. The ready page is geometrically clean. Across every width (320 to 1440 and 844×390), text size (100 to 200%) and both roles, the 16px phone edge, the 16px band-to-card and card-to-card gaps, the 20px text inset, the 40px tiles, chevrons and switches all measure exactly on their row centres (|Δ| ≤ 0.5px), the 12px tile-to-text gap, the 20px right inset of chevrons and switches, the 44px targets and the two columns from 1024 (equal tops, 16px gutter, equal widths) all hold. No sideways overflow in any state. The defects are in the non-ready states and at large text: the "Your details" head line breaks when it carries a state word or action (AL-1), the alerts list's loading rows and error panel are shorter than the ready list whenever a topic wraps, so Sign out moves when the read lands or fails (AL-2), the failed-save line's icon doesn't scale or hang with its text (AL-3), and the details card's hairlines end 20px short of the edge that every other hairline on the page reaches (AL-4).

## Gaps

- The shared preview was under heavy load from the other inspectors (load average 17–22); about 1 in 8 page loads timed out or hit the app's "This page could not be loaded" screen and Firefox navigations were often aborted (`NS_ERROR_ABORT`). Each failed job was retried once or twice; the combinations still missing are listed by omission in the coverage table above. Missing that matter most: Firefox save failed and signed out (no Firefox measurement), and Firefox Manager phone ready. The CSS involved is the same in every engine and the two other engines agree on every number, so I don't expect a Firefox-only geometry defect, but it isn't proven.
- Text size was changed with the root font size on an already loaded page (as the harness does), and widths within a group with `setViewportSize` on the same page. The phone group shares `isMobile`/touch; the desktop group has neither.
- "Alerts on this device" as a button row (`deviceAlerts` true) can't be shown in this preview (the feature flag is off); only the note row was measured. Its markup reuses the same row classes, tile and chevron as the measured rows.
- Reduced motion has no geometric effect on this page (only transitions are removed); not separately measured.
- Keyboard focus rings were read from the CSS, not measured: rows and switches draw a 2px ring inset by 2px (`premium-profile.css:37`), so it can't be clipped by the card.
