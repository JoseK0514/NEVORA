Shift card order

  1. The h2 keeps its id and its 15px eyebrow style at 94% white. Its text ("Your shifts" or "Next shift") is the PM's call (PRD Q2); the look is the same either way. The Published chip stays. No "Now" badge and no new colour: the card infers nothing about clock status.
  2. "Scheduled now" (h3, 17px/600 white) leads, with the plain sentence under it in 15px at 94% white: 5.4:1 at the lightest, top-left point of the gradient. Today's 82% measures 4.44:1 there, so this slice raises all pass secondary text to 94%. "Today" leads the date in white 600, as "Tomorrow" does.
  3. One tear line (dashed rule plus the two page-colour notches) separates now from next, replacing today's plain hairline. One pass, one tear: the card stays the single brand moment (U2).
  4. "Next shift" h3 heads the future block, which keeps the countdown pill. One "View schedule" footer closes the card on a solid 20% white hairline. Both times keep the pass time size, so neither shift looks secondary.
