## Round 1

### Verdict

Colour holds up. Across 58,658 text samples (427 renders, 2,042 analysis passes), no text falls below its WCAG ratio, and axe reports no violations on any Wave A page in Chromium. The lowest measured ratio is 5.14:1 (muted "You give" on the inset shift block). The tinted notices and status pills all clear 5.5:1. The type roles are also right: every phone text is 15px or larger except the 12px chip counts, and every role matches a kit token (390: 30/36, 17/24, 17/22, 15/20; 1440: 32/40, 16/24, 16/22, 14/20). There are no straight apostrophes or quotes, no "..." and no blame words ("missing", "missed", "failed to") on screen. The defects come from wrapping and naming:

- At large text, a person's name on Team availability and the coverage need "1 more person needed" are cut off with an ellipsis. At 320/200% the need breaks inside a word ("1 / mor / e…").
- On the Time line of the summaries, the "·" before a duration starts a new line, and the duration "8h 30m" itself splits at 768/200%. A weekday also breaks away from its date.
- Some things go by more than one name: one location is "Main Office" or "Northside Main Office" (both on the same form), a shift's lead is "Shift lead" or "No designated Lead", and the same reasons and pending state are worded differently on different pages.
- Two summaries say something the card below them contradicts.
- Two small type slips: the step lists are 15px instead of 17px on phones, and the copy receipt's reference code is in a second typeface.

Count: 2 P1 and 12 P2. Two of the P2s come only from the source, because the preview has no pending shift request (TC-WA-12, and the manager half of TC-WA-13).

Evidence was taken on the frozen preview, which runs commit `6371f7e`. The worktree has since moved to `9ee1ee8` (`dab5869`, `4bfad86`, `157e25f`, `cdf714d`). I checked the diff: none of those commits touch the lines behind these findings, and every `file:line` below is the worktree as it is now.
