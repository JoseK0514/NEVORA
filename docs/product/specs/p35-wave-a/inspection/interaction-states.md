# Wave A inspection: interaction and states

Inspector: interaction and states (prefix IS-WA). Read only: no code, CSS, test or data was changed, nothing was committed, and every POST was held in the browser and then aborted (`page.route` → `abort`), so no submission reached the server. Screenshots and scripts are in the session scratchpad under `inspect/interaction-states/` (paths below are relative to it: `…/scratchpad/inspect/interaction-states/`).

## Round 1

October 19, 2026. Preview `127.0.0.1:50447`, branch `claude/p35-wave-a-1283c8` at `6371f7e`.

**Verdict.** Keyboard order, focus rings and the semantics of the kit pages are in good shape: every Wave A page has one h1, a named Back that lands where it says, a visible 2px focus ring on every stop (white on the band, teal on cards), and a correct Details/Scheduling tablist. Where the pages fall short is what happens when things go wrong. A failed Approve, Decline or Delete replaces the whole page with the generic "This page could not be loaded" screen. A failed Save, Add, Send or Offer puts its message under the phone tab bar (390) or below the fold (1440), and focus drops to the page body, so the person sees the button go back to "Save shift" and nothing else. The shared inline validation turns the Wave A help text into garbled messages ("Enter employee idunique. they use it to sign in."). The loading placeholders on the form pages show the Schedule grid or the Team list. **4 P1 and 8 P2, no P0.** Not tested because the preview has no data for them: Shift requests Approve, Decline and Close request, Save replacement, the swap choice radio group and Send swap request (see Gaps).

**Environment note.** The dev server was under heavy load from the parallel inspectors (load average 12–19, page loads of 11–41 s). About one load in four came back as a 500 caused by `canceling statement due to statement timeout`, which showed the root "This page could not be loaded" page. Those 500s come from the database timeouts, not from Wave A. Every check below that met one was repeated until the page loaded, or is listed under Gaps.

### Checked and found correct

**Keyboard order and focus** (Chromium, Tab from the top of the document, every stop recorded; 390: all 23 manager routes × Manager and Owner plus 6 employee/lead routes; 1440: Add shift, Availability, New employee, Clocker PIN, Swap, Unable to work; the others hit load 500s at 1440 and are in Gaps):
- The order follows reading order on every page: header, then the named Back in the band, band pills/arrows, the form fields top to bottom, the primary action, then Cancel, then the tab bar. At 1440 Add shift goes through the main column, then Save shift and Cancel in the side column (1034,479 → 1034,543). That is correct for a form whose action sits beside it.
- Focus ring present on 100% of stops (0 stops without a style change): 2px solid `#FFFFFF`, offset 2px, on band controls (Back, pills, week arrows); 2px solid `#077572`, offset 2px, on card controls, fields, checkboxes and primary/secondary buttons; offset 1px on textareas; offset −2px on tab bar items. Focus is never fully hidden at 390 or 1440. Partial clipping only on the chip (IS-WA-6) and a 15px overlap of the Unable to work textarea by the tab bar (the shell's scroll-padding: noted, not filed).
- One h1 on every page and state (counted visible h1: 1 on all 52 records at 390). The heading order is h1 → h2 card titles, with no skipped level.
- Landmarks: `main`, `header`, `nav "Mobile navigation"`, `nav "Requests pages"`, `nav "Time off sections"` / `"Shift request sections"`, `nav "Choose what to fill"` (Coverage), `aside "Shift summary"` (Add shift), and card `section`s named by their titles. No unnamed controls, duplicate ids or dangling `aria-describedby`/`aria-controls` on any page.

**Back links land where they name** (href checked, and the destination's h1 checked for each): Add shift → `/app/schedule?week=2026-10-19` "Schedule"; Edit shift → `/app/schedule?…&day=2026-10-19#schedule-employee-AUDIT-E`; Coverage → Schedule; Copy receipt → Schedule; Team member → Team; New employee → Team; Clocker PIN → the person (`/app/employees/AUDIT-E`, label "Alexandra Rivera"); Swap → "Shift requests" (`?tab=requests`, lands on "Your requests"); Unable to work → "Shift requests" (`?tab=my`, lands on "Your requests"). The two employee forms use different tab parameters for the same tab, which is harmless.

**Details/Scheduling tablist** (Chromium 390; WebKit 390; Firefox 390): `role=tablist "Employee profile sections"`, roving tabindex (selected 0, other −1). ArrowRight → Scheduling (panel shown, focus moves), ArrowRight again wraps to Details, End → Scheduling, Home → Details, ArrowLeft wraps to Scheduling. Tab from the tab goes into the panel's first field, and Shift+Tab returns to the selected tab and then to Back "Team". Click works too. The panels are `tabpanel` named by their tab, and the hidden panel is `hidden`. All three engines behave the same.

**Disclosures** (Delete shift, Reference, How a swap works, How the order is chosen; Chromium; Delete shift also WebKit and Firefox): Enter opens, Space toggles, Tab after opening goes into the content (for example "Delete this shift"). Escape doesn't close a native `<details>`, which matches the platform (APG disclosure needs only Enter/Space), so it isn't filed. The chevron transition is off under reduced motion (`premium-wave-a.css:139-141`).

**Filter chips** (Availability): `role=group "Show"`, each a `button` with `aria-pressed`, and exactly one pressed after each Enter/Space. Names "Everyone , 7", "Not submitted , 7", "Submitted , 0", "Unavailable , 0". The polite status "7 people shown" updates to "0 people shown", and the empty state reads "No one matches this filter." with "Show everyone".

### Gaps (not checked, and why)
