### Verdict

Not at zero. The round-1 build has three widths-and-roles defects of its own, all in the same family: things placed side by side by viewport width alone stop fitting once the field or the text is wider than the space. The worst is **WR-WA-1**: field pairs go two-up at 400px, so at 430 (a common phone) New employee's "Primary location" reads "Choose a locati" and the member's saved email reads "audit-long@exam" at default text size, and it gets worse at 125–200%. The other two are P2: the "Not submitted" pill on Team availability loses its last letter at 320 with 200% text, and a long name in the Person select is cut through a letter with no ellipsis. I also measured six defects that other areas already filed (AL-WA-1, AL-WA-2, AL-WA-3, TC-WA-1, TC-WA-2, TC-WA-6) and the role gap IS-WA-9; I confirm them with my own numbers below and don't refile them. All seven have fixes in the worktree that the preview doesn't run yet, so they need the round-2 check. Everything else held: **no sideways page scroll in any of 1,279 Chromium measurements** (319 page loads × up to four text sizes), the same in WebKit and Firefox where run, landscape keeps the phone layout with nothing clipped, every kit value (card, band, button, pill, link, field) matches the Phase 2 and 3 pages, and every role lands where it should.
