### Verdict

The phone geometry of the Wave A pages is exact: across 512 phone rows (320–430 and 844×390, text 100–200%, Chromium) every card sits 16px from both page edges, cards and stacked blocks are 16px apart, card text starts 20px in, label-to-field and help-under-field are 8px everywhere (1,272 and 410 measurements), fields are 48px with radius 12, buttons 52px with radius 12, every side-by-side pair (Decline/Approve, Cancel/Save shift, Cancel/Save changes, Cancel/Add employee, Cancel/Save PIN, Cancel/Send report) shares top, height and baseline to 0px, and no touch target is under 44px. Firefox and WebKit at 390 and 1440 match Chromium. The defects are in two places. First, the **side column on Add shift and Coverage is sized in rem with a 22rem maximum**, so as text grows it takes the main column's width: at 1024 and 200% text the main column is **0px wide** (the Add shift form and the Coverage candidates vanish), and at 1440/200% the form is 368px next to a 704px summary. Second, a handful of **fixed-pixel marks don't follow the text** they sit beside (checkboxes and notice icons drift up to 9.5px off their first line at 200%), the Availability stat label **"Unavailable all week" runs past the card edge at 320**, the **Details/Scheduling switch spills out of its track at 320/200%**, the **720px reading width sits 14px in from the band at 1024**, and in the two-up Time off grid at 1440 neighbouring cards' Decline/Approve rows step 47px apart when their facts differ. Two P0, two P1, five P2.
