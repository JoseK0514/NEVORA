# Wave A inspection: bars and stability

## Round 1

October 19, 2026. Inspector: bars and stability (read only). The preview at the `access.json` URL was frozen at the round-1 build (worktree `6371f7e`). The worktree has since moved to `0a69fb3` with round-1 fixes from other areas. That build was not synced to the preview, so none of its changes were measured here. Where HEAD already touches a finding, the finding says so.

**Verdict.** The bars are sound. On all 26 page variants, at 320, 390 and 844×390, the band joins the shell header (or the section-tab row) with no seam and the same teal, `rgb(28, 74, 71)`. The sticky header stays at the top, the tab bar stays fixed, and the last card ends 20px above the tab bar. The page held still during every held submission I could run (9 forms). The Details/Scheduling switch and every disclosure kept their control in place. Stability is not clean, though. Six findings are open: three P1 and three P2.
- **Loading placeholders.** None of them match their Wave A page. The band jumps by 4 to 122px when the page arrives.
- **Font swap.** Inter loads late with `font-display: swap`, which re-wraps band text. Add shift at 390 shifts by CLS 0.022, and Edit shift at 768 by CLS 0.15.
- **Team availability filters.** Choosing a filter that shortens the list throws the chip row 411px down under the finger.
- **P2s.** Band pills fade and clip when they actually fit. Short pages with section tabs scroll 36px over nothing. At 200% text the last card sits 5px above the tab bar.

Method: Playwright with Chromium as the main browser, plus WebKit and Firefox spot checks. One browser instance at a time. Each load used a fresh context, so the browser cache started cold. I used a buffered `PerformanceObserver('layout-shift')` from document start, and I measured geometry at the top and with the page scrolled to the end. Submissions held every POST for 2.5s and then aborted it in the browser. Placeholders were captured during a real client navigation from Inbox, using the dev server's own latency. Scripts are in `…/scratchpad/inspect/stability/`. Sweep data is in `res-final.jsonl` (175 page × role × width loads, all ready). The shared dev server timed out its database under the other inspectors' load. Failed loads ("This page could not be loaded") were discarded and retried; they are not Wave A defects.

### Checked and found correct (preview build `6371f7e`)

- **Band joins the shell header on phones.** On all 26 page variants (every page in the brief, including error and Owner-only states), at 320, 390 and 844×390 in Chromium, the band's top equals the bottom of whatever is above it, with 0px gap: the header at 68, or the section tabs at 112 on Time off, Shift requests and Availability. Header, tab row and band are all `rgb(28, 74, 71)`. Spot checks at 390 in WebKit (Add shift, Time off, Availability, Swap) and Firefox (Add shift, Shift requests, New employee) give the same numbers. At 200% the header is 78, the tabs 78–129 and the band starts at 129 (0px gap).
- **Section tabs on the band.** Each row is 44px tall. The current tab is correct on each page (Time off, Shift requests, Availability) at every width. The row scrolls with the page and the header stays sticky at top 0 when scrolled to the end. At 320 the row truly overflows: Availability ends at 323. The selected tab stays whole, and "Time off" is cut 2–3px at the left (section-tabs is shell-owned; noted, not filed).
- **Tab bar.** It is fixed at 776–844 at 320 and 390, and 322–390 at 844×390. The last content ends 20px above it (756 vs 776) on every long page at 100%. On desktop (768, 1440) the header is sticky at 0–65 and there is no tab bar.
- **CLS, load to ready, cold cache.** Measured on 175 loads. Every page not listed in ST-WA-2 stays at or below **0.005**, and most are 0 to 0.0005. Largest of the rest: Swap (Lead) 390 0.0048, Unable to work 320 0.0036, Swap 320 0.0026, New employee 1440 0.0024, Clocker PIN (Owner notice) 768 0.0019, Team availability 320 0.0010. Member (both panels server-rendered, one hidden on hydration) stays at 0–0.0002, because the hidden panel sits below the visible one. Per-page numbers are in `res-final.jsonl`.
- **The page doesn't move while a submission is pending** (390 unless noted; POST held 2.5s then aborted). The button's top, width and height and the document height were identical before and during the hold:

  After failure, the message appears below the actions (document +88px, +196px for the callout), so nothing above the button moves.
- **Add shift side column at 1024+.** It is not sticky (`position: static`), and doesn't need to be. At 1024×768, Save is at 712–764 at the top (inside 768) and 327–379 at the maximum scroll of 385. At 1440×900, Save is at 712–764, then 479–531 at the maximum scroll of 233. Save and the summary are always on screen.
- **Details/Scheduling switch** (Member, Owner, 390 and 1440, normal and reduced motion). The switch stays at y=160 in both directions, with no layout-shift entries. The document goes 1682 ↔ 1233 at 390 and 1355 ↔ 1194 at 1440, and scrollY is unchanged (144 / 149).
- **Disclosures.** Edit shift "Delete shift": the summary stays at 704 while the document grows 136px below it. Copy receipt "Reference" (Owner and Manager): stays at 626, no layout change. Coverage "How the order is chosen": the summary stays at 495. With normal motion the content below opens over 200ms in 5 steps (`portal-motion.css:80-85`). With reduced motion it opens in one step (`:143`).
- **Reduced motion.** The `.wa-chip`, `.wa-segment` and `.wa-option` transitions and the disclosure caret are set to `none` under `prefers-reduced-motion` (`premium-wave-a.css`). Measured on the chips and segments: same positions and no animation.
- **Placeholders under the section tabs** start at the tabs' bottom (112) with the same teal, so the tab row itself never moves during loading.

### Not covered this round (gaps)

- **Choosing a different swap option:** the preview has no swap targets for `AUDIT-E` or `AUDIT-LEAD` (0 `.wa-option`). This is the Codex "swap choose state" item.
- **Shift requests decision cards (Approve/Decline/Close pending) and the Team activity history disclosure:** the preview has no pending, coverage or history items.
- **Desktop placeholders (768+):** the placeholder band is hidden there by design (`portal-premium.css:437-438`), so I didn't compare it.
- **Widths 360, 430 and 1024** were not swept, apart from 1024×768 for the side column.
- **CLS at 200% text:** couldn't be measured cleanly, because the root font size was set at DOMContentLoaded (this gave artifact shifts of 0.06 and 0.03 on two pages). Geometry at 200% was measured.
- **WebKit and Firefox** have no layout-shift API, so only geometry was checked there. No real iOS or Android device was used.
