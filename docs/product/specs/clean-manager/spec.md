## Visual direction (v3, approved)

Jose rejected two earlier rounds as packed (5/10), then as looking generated ("80% AI"). What was removed, and must not come back: dark gradient hero cards, glass and blur, glows and grain, giant decorative numbers, donut charts used as decoration, pastel gradient avatars, "Good morning" greetings, pills on every row, and cards with shadows inside cards.

What the approved screens do instead:
- **Phones follow iOS conventions exactly.** Large title, inset grouped lists on the grouped grey, 0.5px separators, a standard tab bar (filled icons, tint on the active tab, red badge), segmented controls, half-height sheets with Done. Android gets the same structure with Material conventions in the build.
- **Desktop is flat and quiet,** in the manner of professional tools: a light sidebar, a 52px top bar with the page's actions, 1px hairlines instead of shadows, underline tabs, 10px radius boxes, tables for tabular data, and split view (list plus detail) for the Inbox.
- **Type: the platform's system face** (SF Pro on Apple devices, Segoe UI on Windows, Roboto on Android). This replaces Inter in the app; Inter stays for marketing pages if wanted. Sizes follow the iOS text styles on phones (34 large title, 20 section, 17 body, 15 subhead, 13 footnote).
- **Colour: NEVORA teal is the app tint** (actions, links, the active tab, selection). Red only for lateness and open shifts; amber only for waiting and needs-review. Everything else is black and the system greys. Monogram avatars are the system grey.
- **Content over decoration.** Home shows who is working now and who starts next instead of charts. Numbers sit right-aligned in tabular figures.
- **The wordmark** stays in the desktop sidebar. On phones it lives in the app icon and launch screen, not on every page.

These replace the "unchanged constants" for font and wordmark placement below; the tint colour, Phosphor icons, 44px targets, WCAG 2.2 AA and reduced motion stay.

## Why

QAOWNER Home runs to three phone screens with eight sections. It shows five counts that don't agree (28, 28, 22, 29 and 6), the same "no clock-in" problem four times, red on nearly every card, and rarely used recovery tools on the daily page. The page inventory found the same habits across the Manager and Owner app:
- The same count on several pages.
- Tools reachable from three or four places.
- Two stacked rows of pills.
- A footnote under most cards.
- Subsets shown as if they were totals.
- No phone path to the request lists.

## The clean contract (every page)

1. **One job per page.** Each page answers one question (below). Anything else moves to the page that owns it.
2. **One number per idea.** A count appears once per screen and means the same everywhere (the count dictionary, below). A subset is never labelled like a total.
3. **Calm by default.** Coral (urgent) only on the one thing to act on now: the Next up card and Inbox "Act now" rows. Amber for "needs review". Grey for everything else.
4. **Less text.** A helper line stays only if it prevents a mistake. Definitions go behind an info disclosure. Payroll and legal wording stays word for word.
5. **One home per tool.** One primary place, at most one shortcut.
6. **Same thing, same pattern.** One row, one filter row and one status vocabulary everywhere.

Unchanged constants: wordmark, teal actions, the status ladder, Inter and Phosphor, 44px targets, WCAG 2.2 AA, reduced motion. **Every old URL keeps working.**

## Page by page: one job, what stays, what goes

### Home ("Today"): "What needs me now, and how is today going?"
**Approved, top to bottom (phone):**
1. Large title **"Today"** with the date above it. Search and the bell (a dot, no number) in the navigation bar.
2. **Owner setup row** (Owner only, until done): "Finish setting up · Next: weekly hour targets · 1 of 2".
3. **Needs you 10** (link: Inbox): three rows with counts: Not clocked in 6, Open shifts today 3, Time-off request 1. Each opens its Inbox filter.
4. **Working now 2:** who is clocked in, where, since when and until when.
5. **Later today 1:** who starts later.

**Desktop:** "Today" title; Needs you and Working now/Later today side by side; below, a full-width **Shifts today** timeline (clocked in, not clocked in, later, and a "now" line).

**Moves out:**
- This week → Schedule.
- Plan ahead → Inbox.
- Team tools (Attendance, Team overview, Schedule activity, Clocker recovery, Account recovery) → More › Team tools, and the desktop sidebar under Team.
- The "Published shifts only" footnote is dropped.

**Target:** about 1,300px on a phone (today 2,490). On desktop, one column of actions on the left and Today on the right; nothing below the fold at 1440.

### Home's other views (Attendance, Team overview, Activity)
- **Attendance:** keeps today's clock-ins. Correction requests move out (the Inbox owns decisions). The second "Clocker recovery" link goes.
- **Activity:** shows what changed (published, edited, decided), not open to-dos.
- **Team overview:** unchanged, minus the third "Manage targets" link.

### Inbox: "What do I need to decide?"
- Filters stay: All, Act now, Requests, Plan ahead (a segmented control on phones, underline tabs with counts on desktop).
- Sections: Not clocked in, Open shifts today (grouped by location, not one row per gap), Requests (with an "All requests" row), Plan ahead (open shifts later this week as one row with the count, hour targets, availability deadline, earlier this week). A footnote says planning items don't count toward today's number.
- **Phone:** tapping an item opens a half-height sheet: shift, location, role, today's activity, and two actions (Edit Shift, Open Timecard).
- **Desktop:** split view. The list on the left, the selected item's detail on the right with the same facts and actions.
- "Earlier this week" folds into Plan ahead as a closed disclosure.
- The desktop "Request pages" card goes; the Requests view's last row replaces it on every width.
- Notes shrink to one line or go. The availability-deadline note points to where the setting lives.

### Schedule: "Plan and publish this week"
- The band keeps "1 of 2 locations published · 21 shifts".
- **Takes Home's "This week"** as one row: "Availability received 0 of 8".
- **Toolbar:** week stepper and More. Templates and This week appear once each.
- **Location chips:** state words only, without repeating the published count.
- **Stats:** People assigned · Hours assigned. Shifts goes (it's in the band).
- **Build and publish:** Publish buttons only for unpublished locations.
- The availability-deadline form moves to Scheduling rules.

### Time off, Shift requests and Availability: "Decide requests"
- One pill row (band).
- **Availability:** the "This week" stat card goes; its chips already carry the counts. The deadline line moves into the band summary.

### Team: "Find a person"
- The band says "Team" and "N people". The active count shows once (the filter pill keeps it).

### Timecards: "Approve this period's time"
- Phone: a This week / Last week control, then **Needs review** (each row says why, for example "Missing clock-out") and **All timecards** with hours on the right. Select is in the navigation bar.
- Desktop: four figures (Timecards, Need review, Approved of 13, Recorded), underline tabs, and one table with checkboxes. Per-row action cards go; actions live in the detail and in bulk selection.
- The default period is the current Monday to Sunday (the same as the Inbox), so "Need review" matches.
- The period chooser becomes one compact row: "Oct 19 – Oct 25 ›".
- The long confirmation paragraph (legal wording, unchanged) shows only in the confirm step.

### Insights: "How did the period go?"
- Phone: Week / Month / Custom, one chart card in the style of Apple Health (total recorded time, bars by day), hours by person, then the period figures. Export in the navigation bar.
- Metrics with no data are hidden instead of showing "Not available".
- At a glance comes first.
- The duplicate "Period summary" goes.
- Export becomes one menu (CSV, Print or Save PDF).
- Definitions live in the existing "Report definitions" disclosure only.

### Updates and Notifications
- One status label per row.
- The footnote lines go.
- Notifications' lead paragraph becomes one link: "Decisions are in your Inbox".

### Owner only: Payroll and the Settings hub
- The same contract applies. **All payroll wording stays exactly as approved.**
- The Settings hub groups use the standard row.

## Count dictionary (each number, once)

| Number | What it counts | Where it shows |
|---|---|---|
| **Needs you** | Unclocked shifts today + coverage gaps **today** + waiting requests (10 in the mockups; today's build shows 28 because every gap this week counts) | Home "Needs you", Inbox badge (tab bar and sidebar), Inbox title line. |
| **Act now** | Unclocked shifts today + coverage gaps today | Inbox "Act now" filter only |
| **Requests** | Waiting time off, swaps, pick-ups, "Unable to work" | Inbox "Requests" heading; the band of each request page |
| **Plan ahead** | Non-urgent upcoming items **plus** earlier-this-week items | Inbox "Plan ahead" heading only (Home no longer shows it) |
| **Locations published** | Published of all active locations, this week | Schedule band only |
| **Availability received** | Submitted of employees and leads, this week | Schedule "This week" row; Availability band |
| **Timecards needing attention** | Current Monday to Sunday | Timecards overview only |

What changes for counts in code:
- Coverage gaps after today move from the urgent section to Plan ahead (`inboxSections` in `src/lib/action-center/inbox.ts`, a client-side sort), so Needs you counts today only. If Codex considers the server's URGENT priority authoritative, this becomes a contract note in `CODEX.md` instead.
- Home's "Showing 3 of N" goes.
- Home's "Plan ahead N" goes.
- Home stops letting `?week` change the today count.
- The Timecards default period becomes Monday to Sunday.

All UI; no server read changes.
