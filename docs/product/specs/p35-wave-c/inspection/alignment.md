# Wave C inspection, round 1: alignment and geometry

October 19, 2026 · Inspector: alignment and geometry (prefix AL-) · Preview `http://127.0.0.1:49855`, fictional data, Owner `QAOWNER` on every page and Manager `AUDIT-M` on Account recovery and Clocker recovery. Read only: nothing edited, nothing saved, no Sign out.

## Verdict

At 100% text the Wave C frame is precise. On all 13 routes, at all 8 widths and both roles, cards sit on the 16px page edge, card text starts 20px in, the band-to-card overlap is constant, the gap between stacked blocks is 16px, every field is the same height with its label 8px above and its help 8px below, and every button is 52px with its label centred. The defects show up in three places. **Large text (200%):** button labels are `nowrap`, so three primary or secondary labels spill out of their buttons. Features & words' two-column choices clip words at 1024. Branding's preview tabs overlap. The Clocker link box breaks "/clocker/2" mid-word and stretches its Copy button to 176px tall. A hub row description runs 7px past the card edge. **Desktop:** the Settings hub's two-column grid leaves 84–124px holes between cards. **Small precision:** the location card head pill sits off the title baseline, a disclosure caret centres on two lines, band "Add" controls wrap, and the footnote and "No changes" lines are 4px off every edge. Count: **0 P0, 6 P1, 6 P2.**

Method: Playwright with the saved sessions. Each page was loaded once per context and resized across the widths, then measured at root font size 100/125/150/200%. The landscape context was fresh, because resizing into landscape doesn't reproduce the phone shell. Boxes come from `getBoundingClientRect`, text lines and baselines from Range client rects (baseline = rect top + 0.8 × content height for Inter), and styles from `getComputedStyle`. The server was overloaded during the run (500s and "didn't load" cards), so every degraded cell was re-run until it rendered ready. Scripts: `.../scratchpad/inspect/alignment/` (`measure.js`, `heads.js`, `matrix.mjs`, `crops.mjs`, `states.mjs`). Raw data: `data/*.json`. Crops: `crops/`. Coordinates are CSS px.
