Sheet

  1. Full screen white sheet: width:100vw; height:100dvh; max-height:none; margin:0; border:0; border-radius:0, top padding env(safe-area-inset-top). No backdrop shows. No open or close animation (U8).
  2. Field: --g-inset fill, 8px radius, 44px tall, 17px text (no iOS zoom), 20px muted glyph. Phone placeholder "Search" (the long desktop placeholder needs 264px and gets cut at 390). Clear button once there is text: Phosphor XCircle fill 20px muted in a 44px target at the field's right edge. Focused field gets a 1px teal border plus 1px teal ring. "Close" is a tertiary text button: teal 17px/500, 56×44 target. Accessible name "Close search".
  3. Group labels: 15px/600 muted, sentence case on phones (desktop keeps 12px uppercase). The 15px floor applies.
  4. Rows: 48px minimum, 8px radius, label 17px ink, detail 15px muted, wraps on long names (no ellipsis). The keyboard hint row is hidden.
  5. Highlighted row (aria-selected): --g-accent-wash with --g-accent-strong 600 label (6.50:1). Pressed row: --g-accent-pressed (5.87:1 for the label).
