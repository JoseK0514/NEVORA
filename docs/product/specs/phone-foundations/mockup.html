One button shape

  1. Primary: --g-accent fill, white 17px/600 label (5.53:1), 8px corners (--g-control-radius), 44px minimum, 10px/16px padding, line-height 1.25. Pairs sit in a wrapping row (flex: 1 1 max-content): when two labels can't share a line, each button takes its own full row instead of wrapping its label.
  2. Secondary: white, 1px --g-border, ink label. "Refresh timecards" shows its existing short label "Refresh" below 390; accessible name stays "Refresh timecards".
  3. Pending keeps the size; only the label changes ("Approving…"), button locked, no spinner. Disabled: opacity .55 (existing rule).
  4. Full-width primary on phones (sticky confirms, sheet actions, offline "Try again"): 48px. Every other button: 44px. Pressed: hover colour plus scale(.98) over 120ms; no scale under reduced motion.

Chosen day off teal

  1. Track: the D4 segmented track (--g-inset, 1px #E6E7E9, 4px padding). Two-line segments use 8px corners inside a 12px track (concentric), not the one-line pill.
  2. Chosen: white, ink, weight 600, D4 shadow, plus a 1px inset ring in --g-muted. The ring is the cue that meets WCAG 1.4.11: 5.64:1 against the track, 6.05:1 against the white fill. The white fill alone is 1.07:1. A hairline divider separates "Full week" from the days.
  3. Today: ink date at 600 and a 4px ink dot under it; keeps the dot when chosen. Shape, not colour, carries "today"; aria-label keeps ", today". No teal anywhere in the picker.
