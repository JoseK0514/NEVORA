## Upscales confirmed

- Time facts are grey meta: "Until 7:00 PM" on Home's shift card and "Now", "Tomorrow" on Schedule were teal chips.
- Time's Right now header is a quiet "● Clocked in" with the 6px green dot (was a teal chip); "In progress" is grey words.
- Standalone green chips lead with the 12px check: Last week's "Approved", Pay's "Statement ready".
- Desktop week card: "Now" and "Lead" on today's teal block are white band chips, crisper than the 18% glass chip.
- Calendar's "Keep the link to yourself" is the teal privacy callout (ShieldCheck, 600 title, 1px edge), replacing an amber
  lock in a grey box; Help's safety note is the same callout with Support's title.
- Home's to-do circles carry meaning: amber for Action needed, teal for the rest.
- Time off is CalendarMinus and Can't work is UserMinus across Requests and Notifications; Help on More is the question
  mark, matching the sidebar.
- Pick up and Requests' Review are 44px tinted pills at every width; "At least 7" stays one 20px pill at 320.
- Shell: Help 20px on the entries' column, a round sidebar toggle, no half-cut rail entry at 844.

## Not reviewable from pictures

- Hover, keyboard order, focus rings, reduced motion, High Contrast, 200% text and screen readers.
- Real phones: the 390 and 320 captures are a desktop browser (fine pointer), so a coarse-pointer phone may draw some
  36px buttons differently; the 844 captures show the coarse rules do change heights (finding 2).
- States the fixture does not reach: "Still clocked in" amber callout, On break, "Not clocked in · N min", the Pay
  statement footer, device-alert results, the restrictions conflict glyph, loading, error, offline and partial notices.
- Spanish signed-in pages (not captured by design).
- The scan lists two or more `h1` elements on most routes (for example Home, Time, Profile); whether the extras are hidden
  presentations cannot be told from pictures and needs a DOM check.

## Verdict

The pass did what it set out to do on the surfaces it touched: normal states went quiet, chips now mean "look here",
callouts read as one family and most glyphs agree, and each of those is a clear step up from the base. What keeps the
Employee and Lead screens short of Jose's bar is geometry rather than colour. On tablets and the sideways phone, the
most important button on Home and Requests sits 10px off its text edge, rows built outside the kit row hang their
circles outside the card's edge, Schedule's Confirm changes height three times across devices, and the same task still
wears different glyphs and tints from page to page. None of it is broken, and every item has a small, local fix (one
padding token, one height, a handful of glyph and tone props). Fix 1 to 8 before promoting to Development; 9 to 11 are
worth doing in the same round; the polish rows can wait for Jose's call.
