## Verdict

The premium pass did what it set out to do on the status layer: Home, Team, Timecards and Things to do now speak in quiet grey for normal states and keep colour for exceptions, the glyphs are mostly one per meaning, and the shell is tidier than the base. It is not ready to call premium yet. One regression from this pass breaks a real screen (the person page's status collides at 390 and clips at 320), and the phone build still ships 36px and 32px touch targets on four pages the ladder covers. Below that sits a layer of inconsistency a careful designer sees in minutes: the same Prepare button in three heights, a lopsided 682px pill on Home at 900, a time fact still drawn as a chip on Payroll's phone band, "working now" on Team, a Pause glyph outside the set, a thin chevron on Notifications, three divider styles in one card, a band at 270 among bands at 296, and ranges and separators that break at the wrong place on phones. Fix findings 1 and 2, then 3 to 21, and recapture; most are one rule or one prop each.
