## Verdict

The premium pass did its own job on these pages. The Spanish Privacy note Jose pointed at is now a clean titled callout at every width, Support's note sits where it belongs with its sentence intact, Sign in's notes and every field error share one anatomy, and the tablet header fix is a clear upscale. The final tree is not yet at the bar Jose set, because of what surrounds that work. The desktop footer fix traded an overlap for 119 to 254px of empty canvas and pushes Spanish Sign in's help and legal line under the fold at 1440x900; that is new in this tree and should be fixed before the development release is called verified. Two older defects also block: fields and the Sign in button vanish under High Contrast, and on phones the Access help titles sit 37px off the card edge, the same bug just fixed on tablets. After those three, the largest visible gap is consistency: recovery and setup screens still use four note styles next to Sign in's callout, finish marks align two ways on desktop, and Spanish wraps leave single-word lines. Recommend fixing 1 to 3, moving the recovery notes onto `BCallout`, then recapturing the final tree with the missing states (Sign in banners, Forgot, Reset and Set up errors) at 768, 1024 and 1440x800 as well.
