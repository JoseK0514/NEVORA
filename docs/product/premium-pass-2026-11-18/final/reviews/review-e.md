## Verdict

The kiosk is sound where it matters most: every flow completes in both languages at both tablet sizes, no state shows a
done mark it has not earned, axe is clean on 46 states, and the 200% text and High Contrast fixes (D34 to D36) hold. It
is not yet at the premium bar, and the gap is the one Jose named: one glyph per meaning. The kiosk never received
decision 2 or the product's glyph map, so "not confirmed" still wears the retry arrow, red and amber swap their warning
glyphs, the band and the card disagree on red or amber for the same outage, and the blocked cards use a second anatomy.
Those four rows (1 to 3 and 5) come down to one icon map, one band tone and one card pattern, a small change that would
make the kiosk read as one design with the app. After them, fix the stale "Return to PIN", the live Clock in under an
offline note, the help card's result, the raw UUIDs and the portrait PIN card that touches the bottom edge, and the
kiosk can sign off.
