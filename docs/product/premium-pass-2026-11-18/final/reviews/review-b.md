## Verdict

The pass did what it set out to do on the lists: quiet grey for normal states, amber chips with a hairline for exceptions, one count, one glyph for timecards, time off and pay, the privacy callout on Help, and 44px row actions. Those changes read as a real upscale over the base, and Home, Things to do, Team and Schedule now speak one language at 1440 and 900. The pages around them have not caught up. The PINs strip sitting on the band's edge, the More profile row that misses its own column, Restrictions' 36px pills with a quiet button in the disabled fill, three different heights for a full-width primary, "5 working now" beside "Clocked in 5", and an upload arrow on a download link are the kind of misses Jose asked this review to catch, and each takes a small, local fix. The phone layouts at 320 and 390 carry most of the remaining roughness: dangling separators, a split time range, orphaned controls on their own rows, two gutters. On the ladder, the Manager work pages reach Coherent; the settings and account pages sit just under it until rows 1 to 5 and 9 to 14 are fixed. This assesses craft and taste on fictional data; it does not verify usability on real devices, accessibility, or that the backend contracts behave.
