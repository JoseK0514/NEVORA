## Reviewed from the saved work, and what changed

- **Reverted: the added body on the wrong-credentials note** ("Check both and try again." / "Revisa ambos e inténtalo de
  nuevo."). The fields under the note are already marked invalid, the line added a fourth line at 320 and two new strings
  for Jose to approve. The note is now its title alone: "Employee ID or password is incorrect".
- **Changed: the notes' top inset.** Privacy's note sat 18px under the card's top with 20px sides (24 with 28 on a
  desktop); Support's the same. Both now sit as far from the top as from the sides: 20px on phones and tablets, 28px on a
  desktop.
- **Added: the support address in Sign in's deletion note is a link** (`mailto:`), wrapping only after "@", as on the
  policy pages. At 320 it reads "Email support@ / thevelani.com with your …" instead of leaving "Email" alone on a line.
- **Removed dead CSS:** the old teal info-note rules (`.q-auth__alert--info`) that only the session-ended note used.
- **Tried and reverted: the policy date line at 200% text.** Its two lines ("Vigente desde el / 20 de octubre de 2026")
  overlap at 200% text, before and after this package. A rem leading on `.q-doc .q-hd p` (`--b-lh-body-sm`) was added,
  but the capture still measured 20px leading under 29px text, although the rule matched, was `!important` and the token
  read 1.25rem with a 32px root. The cause was not found in the time available, so the change was reverted (it changed
  nothing at the default size either). See "Observed, not changed".
- **Kept:** the Support body wording change and the recovery banners' geometry (see above).
- **Integration check updated:** `src/test/integration/employee-mobile-deletion.integration.ts` read `#login-error` as
  one sentence; it now joins the note's title and body with ". " and compares with `AUTH_COPY.en.loginDeleting`.
