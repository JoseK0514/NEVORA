after real captures showed problems:

  buttons with stronger selectors (8px corners, 15px 500, its own disabled colours). The pill shape, the 14.5/20 600 type
  and the one disabled pair are now also set inside `@layer nv-b` in the module. Save mapping profile keeps the white
  secondary look `payroll-corp.css` gives it (Create export stays the page's one filled button); the module's `.primary`
  now says the same instead of a teal-50 that never rendered. Measured: 999px corners, 44px, disabled `#F0F2F3`/`#8C9397`.
  Evidence `p6-07-*`.
- `.q-deletion__box` on the one checkbox rule was right (24px box with 7px corners became 20px with 6px). Kept, `p6-04-*`.
