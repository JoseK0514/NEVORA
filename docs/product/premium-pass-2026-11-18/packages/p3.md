## Reverted, and why

- **Removing the amber "Waiting" chip from waiting rows** (saved work, Requests and Time off). It reversed EM-023 (Phase 4,
  "one choice for Jose") without Jose's word, and the row loses its answer to "did it go through?". Not a clear upscale,
  so the chips stay. Commit `0cf3c2527`.
- **Green and amber callouts for a normal clock state on Home** (saved work). A normal state got louder, against the
  spec's "colour means look here". Replaced by the quiet kicker described above. Commits `ebafe472a`, `a46181b6a`.
- **"Past shift end · 5 h"** (saved work, Time's header). It was new wording outside the spec's word set; the amber
  callout already says the shift ended. The header keeps "Clocked in" (amber dot) and the copy entries were removed.
- **Help's reply line at the 18px line height** (saved work). It made "Support hours" a 42px target; the line keeps its
  1.5 line height (44px). Commit `19285b1f2`.
- **Amber for "Your manager approves it"** (saved work). It read as a warning before anything was sent; it is teal
  information with the wait glyph.

## Kept as it was, and why

- Pay's two page notes ("Your employer pays you…", "Open a statement to download its PDF…") stay under the cards: they
  speak for the page, and at 1440 putting either inside one of the two side-by-side cards made the pair uneven.
- "Showing your N most recent requests." (corrections) and "Your manager reviews this request…" (correction detail) stay
  where they are: the first card already has a footer, and the second speaks for the whole request.
- The partial-state lines on Notifications and Updates ("Some current notices couldn't be checked…") stay plain notes on
  the canvas; a callout belongs inside a card, and these states cannot be produced on the fictional seed to compare.
- The week card's "Tomorrow" chip (see the table).
