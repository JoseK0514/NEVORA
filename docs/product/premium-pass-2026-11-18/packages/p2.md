## Follow-up (coordinator, Nov 18): one placement rule for a status on a phone row

The first delivery left Team at 390 with every exception chip under the name. Nothing forced that: the title line already
wraps only when the name and the status don't fit. The live dot made an exception chip 12px wider (7px side, 6px dot and
5px gap where the plain chip has an 8px side). Measured on the 390 shots, Carlos Vega's chip starts at x 183 and the title
column ends at about x 329: with the dot the chip would end at x 336, without it at x 326; James Ortiz's row is the same. So both rows grew a
line where nothing was squeezed, which is not a clear upscale.

Change: below 768px, a chip on a row's name line (Team, Things to do, Home's lists, the roster's day list) leaves the live
dot out and keeps the chip's look (22px, its 1px edge, tabular minutes). The row's avatar ring already marks the live
state there (amber: late or not clocked in). The chip then sits on the name's line exactly where the base's did and wraps
under it where the base's did. Kept: the quiet "● Clocked in" keeps its green dot on phones; the chips at a row's end on
tablet and desktop (Team at 900, Home's On now) keep the dot; the kit's drop under the name below 360px is untouched.
This departs from "with the live dot" for phone name lines only, because the dot is exactly what made those rows stop
fitting; the rule is two declarations in `owner-b-home.css` and `owner-b-schedule.css` if Jose prefers the dot and the
taller rows.
