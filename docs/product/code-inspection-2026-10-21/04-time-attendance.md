No critical. 3 high, 5 medium, 4 low. Cost claims in #7 unmeasured.

HIGH — Management cannot close or correct stranded time. clocker_apply_punch is the ONLY time_entries writer (0014:75-172); corrections require the employee as requester, active, entry clocked out (0017:42-52,54-59,78-80,222); inactive employee/location can't punch (pins.server.ts:64-71, devices.server.ts:239-242); updateLocation can deactivate with no open-entry check (locations.ts:44); employee UI offers today+latest 20 entries only (time-corrections.server.ts:154-161). Consequences: deactivated employee / deactivated location / revoked tablet with open entry = open FOREVER; terminated employee's mistakes uncorrectable; open entry blocks timecard approve/finalize (project.ts:108, 0022:35-37); permanently pollutes Attendance Review (500-entry cap) and clock monitor; bites exactly at final pay for a terminated employee. Fix: audited manager "close open entry" command (reason + different-approver); manager-filed corrections (incl. inactive targets); block deactivation with open entry; lift 20-entry window.
2. HIGH — Timecards fail above 300 employee records, inactive count. timecards/service.ts:89-91 no e.active filter; read-model.ts:19 throws >300 cards; maps to read_failed (service.ts:142). No test ≥300 or inactive. Fix: scope to active-or-has-period-data; raise/paginate cap; report as source_limit with explanation.
