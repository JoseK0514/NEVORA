1. HIGH — Nothing stops timecard REOPEN / time changes after payroll approval or export. 0022:27-31 + timecards/service.ts:146-165 REOPEN checks only latest-revision FINALIZE + note; no check of payroll_runs_current APPROVED, native_payroll_ledger, payroll_export_receipts or manifests; drift caught only pre-approval and at period-close manifest/handoff (period-close/service.ts:562-571,757-765). Test gap: only pre-approval reopen tested.
2. 5. 7. 8. SUMMARY: money core in good shape (BigInt + exact rationals, no floats; rounding recorded as evidence; DB-level immutability + CAS fences + worker lease fencing; no pay data in notifications/logs). Weaknesses at the seams: payroll not interlocked with time reopen (#1), population authority in operational scope (#2), correction/export flows lack exactly-once/supersession (#4,#5), heavy read paths (#6,#9). Fixable without touching the calculation core.
