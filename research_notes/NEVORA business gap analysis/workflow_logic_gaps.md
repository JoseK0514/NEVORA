## Key question 1: Which workflows are complete and dependable, and which are half-built or gated in a way that blocks real use?

### Takeaway
The day-to-day core (manual scheduling and publishing, auto-draft, swaps/cover, kiosk punches with Employee ID + PIN, timecard approve/finalize, employee and manager-filed corrections, management close of stranded punches, hours export, and imported-amount pay statements) is built with unusually strong integrity controls, but almost every surface is held behind default-off flags or unverified hosting ("hostedAcceptance: NOT_VERIFIED"). Three workflow families are effectively unusable in a hosted company today: team communications (Updates, conversations, manager reminders) are hard-gated to loopback test databases; staff tasks/checklists and conversations have APIs but no screens; and the Puerto Rico pay rules (daily overtime, meal penalties) are not applied in the default "external" payroll mode.
