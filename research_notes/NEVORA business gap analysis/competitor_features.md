## Q1. How do the leaders compare feature by feature? (benchmark matrix)

### Takeaway
By late 2026 every US leader ships scheduling, mobile clock-in with geofencing, time off, swaps, messaging and payroll export, and most now bundle some AI at no extra charge. The spread between them is in POS-sales forecasting, embedded payroll and pay-access (tips, earned wage access), hiring/training modules, jurisdictional rule engines, and how far their AI "acts" (drafts, contacts staff, prepares payroll) under owner approval.

- AI call-out coverage (Homebase Assistant, Humanity Backfill, Deputy AI) is the newest visible differentiator for small hourly businesses, and it maps directly onto a coffee drive-through's daily pain (sick calls before opening). Our app's existing rules-based coverage ranking (noted in [TRENDS] §3.8) is a starting point; outreach to staff via push after owner approval would be the differentiating step.
- Sales-based forecasting with labor-% targets is a differentiator our app appears to lack (no forecast code found by grep). For a coffee chain, POS-sales data is the obvious input; this is where 7shifts, Toast, Square and Workforce.com are ahead.
- Tips: for coffee drive-throughs, tip pooling is likely relevant; our app has no tip code (grep). Earned wage access stays an "avoid" per [OCT] §5 (fees and regulation), but tip *calculation* and pooling are a separate, lower-risk feature.

### Gaps
- No independent evidence on how accurate any vendor's forecasting or AI scheduling is; all accuracy and time-saving claims are vendor marketing.
- Homebase Assistant pricing and general availability date were not disclosed — [H3](https://siliconangle.com/?p=850756).
- Whether tip pooling matters in Puerto Rico coffee drive-throughs (tip culture, local tip law) was not researched.

### Inferences
- Reliability and trust in time records outweigh feature breadth in reviews: the most-cited negatives are glitches, wrong or lost time, and notifications, not missing features. This supports competing on punch receipts, offline-safe clock-in and calm notification controls ([OCT] §9 items 3, 7, 9).

## Q6. Which competitors support Spanish and serve Puerto Rico; what Spanish-language / LatAm / PR tools exist?

### Takeaway
Most US scheduling apps list Spanish (Homebase, Deputy, 7shifts, Connecteam, When I Work, Square), but Gusto, Rippling and Sling are English-only and no US vendor documents Puerto Rico labor-law presets. Puerto Rico payroll is a weak spot for US SMB platforms: Gusto excludes it, Homebase states 50 states + DC, Square historically excluded PR, Toast says US-only without addressing PR, and Rippling has only a hiring page and one user report. PR employers instead use local payroll bureaus (Alchavo, Easy Payroll) or desktop tax software (AMS, ezPaycheck), plus UKG in the public sector. LatAm Spanish-first tools (Buk, Runa, GeoVictoria, Humand) are strong on attendance, biometrics and local payroll law but none lists Puerto Rico.

### Inferences
- Puerto Rico sits in a gap: US SMB workforce apps have Spanish UI but not PR payroll or Ley 379 presets; LatAm tools have Spanish-first design and local-law engines but for other countries' laws; PR's own providers are payroll bureaus or desktop tax tools with thin scheduling and employee apps. An app that exports cleanly to PR payroll bureaus (Alchavo, Easy Payroll and similar) while handling PR meal/overtime rules and PR Spanish fills that gap.
- Humand and GeoVictoria are the most likely LatAm entrants into PR-like markets: both are Spanish-first, both have US presence (GeoVictoria lists the US and DR; Humand is funding US expansion), and GeoVictoria already ships AI shift planning plus WhatsApp alerts. They are worth watching more than Buk or Runa, whose payroll engines are country-specific.
- WhatsApp alerting (GeoVictoria) is a LatAm-specific pattern absent from US vendors; for Spanish-speaking hourly staff it may matter more than app push.

### Gaps
- No vendor documentation confirms Puerto Rico payroll support for Deputy (Paycor), 7shifts, Homebase (primary), Rippling (primary), Square (current) or Workforce.com.
- Whether US apps' Spanish is Latin American / Puerto Rico Spanish or generic Spanish, and how complete it is per screen, was not tested.
- No PR-based scheduling/time-clock SaaS with an employee app was found beyond Renova (QR attendance) and HiveDesk (US vendor with a PR page); local bureaus' time-clock products (e.g., Alchavo's) were not examined in detail.
- GeoVictoria, Humand, Buk and Runa customer counts and ratings are self-reported; no PR customer evidence found for any of them.
- PR tip-law, biometric-privacy and Ley 289 rest-day rules remain unresearched ([OCT] §10).
